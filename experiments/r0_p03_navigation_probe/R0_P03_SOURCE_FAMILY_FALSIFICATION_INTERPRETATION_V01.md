## Fixed intended source-world question

The falsification target is whether an exact historical source revision, **or a genuinely separately reviewed bounded representation**, could legitimately be considered for prospective disclosure to a **fresh, blind primary information-need author** under Research 513. ## Interpretation needed for a falsifiable, bounded judgment

1. **Exact unit of judgment:** the original rule's tuple of source revision, family, supported proposition, effective time, audience, permitted representation, dependency closure and exposure set. Mixed-content files may require separate claim/representation verdicts. An entire historical file is not safe merely because one historical claim is supported.
2. **Evidence burden:** apply the original rule's eight evidentiary families, ten evidence fields and hard HOLD/disclosure conditions. A Git blob/manifest digest proves byte-level consistency only, not owner authorization or historical execution truth. A historical event or observed report may be truthfully bounded without proving current implementation.
3. **Material missing evidence:** mark it material only when a **specific credible missing source, ambiguity, contradiction, original representation or plausible exposure interaction** could change the claimed proposition, temporal/authority scope, disposition, safe representation or author access. Cite the discriminator and the possible different outcomes. Do not require proof that *no other related document exists anywhere*, and do not auto-reject merely because a source lacks a citation from preliminary orientation files.
4. **HOLD versus exclusion:** use `HOLD_MATERIAL` when a plausible eligible narrow claim remains but a needed discriminator is unavailable; `EXCLUDE_FOR_INTENDED_WORLD` only where the proposed **specific author audience/representation** has a supported exclusion reason, not as shorthand for false history or unknown authority. `RESTRICTED_REVIEW_ONLY` is an independent access restriction that may coexist with either.
5. **Positive dispositions remain provisional:** `CANDIDATE_CURRENT_BOUNDED`, `CANDIDATE_HISTORICAL_BOUNDED` and `CANDIDATE_SANITIZED_DERIVATIVE` require positive case-level evidence and absence of material author-exposure hazards. A sanitized derivative is a **new separately examined artifact**, never an automatic release of a redacted parent. The holdout reviewer may propose this route but cannot perform source release.
6. **False-exclusion attack:** for each HOLD or EXCLUDE, actively attempt the strongest plausible **narrower honest historical claim** or **independently audited sanitized representation** compatible with the same predefined author audience, without inventing missing evidence. If one appears defensible, identify the burden the proposed rule incorrectly prevents or the missing review action that could qualify it. Do not count generic uncertainty as proof of exclusion.
7. **False-admission attack:** challenge each candidate against original-source identity, competent historical authority, competing and later evidence, realization and validation scope, contextual omissions, unsupported representation, old-answer/comparator leakage and combined author-visible exposure. A hard material counterexample blocks author release even when all other paperwork or numerical tallies look favorable.
8. **Reasoned abstention:** the bounded archive may lack material originals or external evidence; record a source-bound HOLD or INDETERMINATE with specific missing evidence. A holdout case that cannot be inspected is not counted as a correct admission, rejection or successful generalization.
9. **No post-hoc adjustment:** this interpretation, the submitted rule and their digests are fixed before holdout disclosure. Any flaw after examining holdout evidence is reported as a **rule failure** or a bounded, separately versioned *future* amendment that must be retested under an independent process, not edited into a retroactive pass.
10. **No population-wide inference:** the stratified 16-case challenge tests contradictions and method reliability only. Even a clean challenge cannot authorize blanket admission of the 2,003 critical historical/validation records or the separate 406 preliminary candidates, and cannot lower task/reference capacity requirements.
