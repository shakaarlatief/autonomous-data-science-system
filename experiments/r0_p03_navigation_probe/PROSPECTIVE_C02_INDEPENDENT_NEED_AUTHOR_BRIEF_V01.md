4. **Exact evidence, authority and recovery:** Investigate the source, authority, provenance or reconstructability of a material historical claim.

Write requests a contributor might naturally ask, **not convenient questions selected because they sound easy to answer**. A request about an actual operational event must not be silently rewritten into a request about a document merely describing a plan.
