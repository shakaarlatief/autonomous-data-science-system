Do not invoke browsers, external search, repositories, linked apps, prior-chat or memory tools, or connected documents. **Stop without originating proposals** if you actually accessed other project/evaluation evidence in this session, know or can see earlier comparison designs, results, specific questions or answer references, or cannot comply with the no-extra-access boundary. The context is historically scoped and incomplete. Do not infer current deployment, final operative authority, or completed execution from a high-level description.

## Candidate needs, not completed tasks

Independently identify up to **twelve distinct, realistic information needs** that a contributor to this project could naturally raise. Attempt three independent needs in each of these **four areas**:

1. **Orientation and situation reconstruction:** Understand the system's responsibilities, state, or progress at a specified historical time.
2. **Project/Product relationships:** Discover a meaningful relationship between building/governing the system and its application or user experience. Both sides must matter.
3. **Impact and evolution:** Understand the consequences, dependencies, changes or limits of decisions over time.
4. **Exact evidence, authority and recovery:** Determine a claim's actual evidential basis, temporal authority, or what would be required to reconstruct a bounded situation.

For each candidate, provide only a plausible contributor's standalone natural-language information request, time scope, user purpose, distinctness from other needs, and a short **NOT_YET_ADJUDICATED** note about possible missing evidence. Do not claim that the needed answer exists. Do not fabricate concrete decisions, event IDs, component names, approvals or execution facts that the orientation does not supply. If three credible needs cannot be originated in an area, report the shortfall. Do not repeat or lightly rephrase prior known questions.

**Do not** answer the requests, specify expected facts, reference source paths or citations, supply relevance labels, propose implementation or retrieval tactics, recommend evaluation arms, create hidden gold, or score anything. All proposals remain unadjudicated until a later separate evidence and authority review.
