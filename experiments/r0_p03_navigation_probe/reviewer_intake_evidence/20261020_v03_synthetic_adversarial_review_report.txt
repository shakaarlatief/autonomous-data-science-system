# Independent adversarial review — R0-P03 source-status reviewer contract V0.3

AMEND — synthetic methodology only

Overall finding: V0.3 substantially strengthens the conceptual source-status methodology, but it does not yet resolve all material release-decision ambiguities within its stated scope.

The strongest improvements concern scoped dispositions, complete-file inspection, treatment of nonnormative historical evidence, temporal applicability, reviewer-only context, and packet-level leakage. However, new and residual ambiguities permit both false candidate admissions and unnecessary exclusions. Several rules can also yield incompatible classifications when applied literally.

Recommendation: AMEND V0.3 prospectively, retain the real-review HOLD, and subject the corrected version to a bounded independent synthetic challenge. This review does not authorize any source admission or scientific experiment.

## 5. Remaining missing dependencies

Beyond individual counterexamples, four methodology-level dependencies require correction.

### 5.1 Materiality needs a reproducible decision criterion

V0.3 requires materiality findings but does not define how reviewers should distinguish:

- Material uncertainty about the truth or scope of a proposed assertion.
- Material uncertainty about what a fresh author could infer from the exposure.
- Nonmaterial uncertainty that can safely remain unresolved.

The words material, confirmed, possible and independently evidenced must remain semantically informed judgments; they cannot be reduced to simple keywords. Nevertheless, reviewers need a common procedural test.

A suitable correction is to require a witness identifying the precise uncertain proposition, its affected exposure, the potential consequence if the uncertainty resolves adversely, and why the uncertainty is or is not material. Where the consequential interpretation cannot be excluded, the affected exposure remains held. Where the uncertainty has demonstrably no bearing on the narrow claim or exposure risk, it need not prevent candidacy.

This would reduce discretionary false admissions without imposing a universal hold for every unknown.

### 5.2 Controlling context needs explicit conflict and coverage rules

Section 5 specifies strong witness fields, including authority, supersession, negation, cross-source qualification and author-world accessibility. It does not fully say how to adjudicate opposing claims when no governing hierarchy is established.&#x20;

The correction should distinguish an evidenced authority hierarchy from a mere document label, and an observed local practice from a universally effective policy. It should also define what constitutes sufficiently complete context searching for a proposed source family and historical assertion.

If the record supports only the existence of incompatible contemporary assertions, that historical disagreement may itself be supportable. It does not justify choosing one assertion as governing without additional evidence.

An unresolved controlling conflict blocks the stronger claim, but need not erase accurately bounded evidence of the conflict's existence.

### 5.3 Source eligibility, packet membership and world sufficiency must remain distinct

The text requires a context-complete author-visible world, but individual candidate evaluation also references already permitted author-visible evidence. This creates the circular-dependency problem demonstrated above.

The corrected method needs at least three distinct decisions:

| Decision                 | Question                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Source or span candidacy | Is this original evidence semantically and contamination-wise eligible, possibly subject to declared co-release dependencies?                 |
| Packet membership        | Can this exact source or dependent bundle be assembled with safe metadata and all required qualifiers?                                        |
| Author-world sufficiency | Does the eventual frozen collection give a fresh author adequate, uncontaminated original evidence for its intended bounded research purpose? |

A packet consisting entirely of individually safe candidates can still fail packet safety or author-world completeness. Conversely, a source can be eligible without having to appear in every future packet.

Section 8 limits the next bounded challenge to V0.2's identified interaction gaps. That is useful for scope control, but V0.3 introduces additional decisions concerning scope types, authorization, bundled dependencies and packet gates. Those new interfaces deserve direct adversarial testing.&#x20;

Required correction: Retain a bounded S0 challenge, but define its scope as V0.3's entire release-interaction decision discipline, including newly introduced rules. The challenge should include positive eligibility, negative contamination, uncertain evidence, compound blockers, conditional co-release, and false-exclusion controls.

A synthetic exit should require that remaining uncertainties are either resolved or demonstrably nonmaterial to methodology safety—not merely that the earlier fictional examples have been answered consistently.

## 6. Candidate soundness, challenge, authorization and author-world construction

This distinction is essential and should become a formal part of the contract rather than an implication spread across Sections 4, 6 and 7.

1. Semantically sound candidate proposal

   The proposed source scope has defensible identity, coverage, evidence-role, materiality, temporal and dependency witnesses. This establishes possible eligibility only. It does not grant author access.
2. Independent challenge and adjudication

   A separately qualified challenger tests the proposal and records acceptance, rejection or unresolved disagreement without overwriting the original evidence. An unresolved material objection must continue blocking exposure. Challenge completion does not by itself authorize release.
3. Actual release authorization

   An explicitly authorized actor or process verifies custody, the adjudicated candidate, exact bytes, declared dependencies, assembled packet, exposed metadata, rendered author interface and relevant publication history. Only successful verification permits the exact bounded release.
4. Complete author-world construction

   The final frozen collection contains sufficient permitted original evidence, necessary controlling context and required family coverage without protected leakage. The collection must itself be independently accepted. Individually authorized sources do not automatically establish a complete world.

V0.3 largely recognizes these separations, but the authorization actor, required sign-offs, unresolved-dissent handling and readback proof are not operationally defined. Its append-only ledger is expressly unimplemented.&#x20;

A prospective correction should include a formal transition scheme with separate semantic, challenge and release states. An `AUTHORIZED` label must not mean merely that a reviewer recommended approval. It must be tied to a specific immutable exposure manifest and an independently evidenced authorization event.

An invalidated dependency must automatically render any dependent authorization unusable until the affected release boundary is rechecked. V0.3's invalidation principle supports this, but the implementation dependency is outstanding.

## 9. Final recommendation

## AMEND

Synthetic methodology

V0.3 is conceptually stronger than the deficiencies it describes, but its candidate/release contradiction, incomplete scope typing, competing primary-status guards and insufficiently specified dependency closure remain material.

The newly constructed examples also demonstrate credible false-admission routes involving dynamic disclosure surfaces, partial temporal authority, unreviewed controlling context and generated metadata, alongside false-exclusion routes involving inert dependencies, coupled source evidence and overly broad uncertainty.

The appropriate disposition is AMEND, not PASS. A wholesale HOLD on further fictional methodological work is unnecessary because the issues can be addressed synthetically without accessing historical evidence.

Recommended boundary decisions:

| Boundary                                                          | Decision          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Continue synthetic contract refinement                            | AMEND — permitted |
| Treat V0.3 as independently accepted methodology                  | Not justified     |
| Begin actual historical source-status inspection                  | HOLD              |
| Make real author-visible source admissions                        | HOLD              |
| Assert completed author-world construction                        | HOLD              |
| Proceed to scored or downstream scientific work under this review | Not authorized    |

The next version should first resolve the P0 contradictions, then demonstrate a deterministic decision model under independent synthetic challenge. It should preserve the important flexibility of V0.3: narrow historical evidence can remain useful even when broader operational authority is unproved, and mixed files need not be excluded indiscriminately.

Bottom line: V0.3 substantially improves the conceptual safety boundary, but it has not yet made that boundary sufficiently deterministic, closed under dependencies, or release-complete to merit a synthetic PASS. Nothing in this review qualifies the methodology for real historical-source inspection or author-world release.
