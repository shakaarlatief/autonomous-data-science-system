## 4. Material finding — Atomic-bundle authorization versus complete author-world freeze

Material stage-order ambiguity

The core M1/M2 repairs survive the direct tests. However, the full V0.5 text leaves one consequential ambiguity at the boundary between independently authorized atomic-bundle disclosure and the separate complete author-world freeze.

This is a textual counterexample, not an observed implementation failure. It affects M1's release path and can also affect an M2-qualified source included in a partial packet.

### 4.1 Exact V0.5 anchors

Path A — Authorization can appear sufficient for disclosure of an atomic bundle

Section 7, line 108:

> “Independently challenge, accept and authorize the exact atomic bundle before disclosure”

Section 9, line 129, defines a release transition from `RELEASE_HOLD` to `AUTHORIZATION_GRANTED`, conditioned on the approved authority, immutable exposure manifest, challenged packet, protected custody, rendering and cumulative-public-history checks.

Section 2, line 37, lists `CONDITIONAL_BUNDLE` and `ASSEMBLED_AUTHOR_PACKET` together and calls author-world sufficiency “additional”.&#x20;

### 4.3 Competing decision paths

Decision path A: Sections 7 and 9 can be read to permit disclosure after atomic-bundle challenge, acceptance and authorization. The exact bundle has obtained `AUTHORIZATION_GRANTED`.

Decision path B: Sections 8, 9 and 11 preserve complete author-world sufficiency as a distinct requirement for S3. The neutral task author should not receive an incomplete or not-yet-accepted author world while the world remains `WORLD_UNFROZEN`.

Both interpretations are possible because V0.5 does not explicitly state whether `WORLD_FROZEN` is a prerequisite to primary task-author disclosure, rather than only to the formal world-freeze outcome.

### 4.4 Why the existing precedence rule does not conclusively close it

Section 1, line 23, expressly governs Sections 3, 5, 7 and 9. It requires independent context acceptance and exact exposure authorization “before any author disclosure or world freeze”. That establishes necessary prerequisites for both events, but does not specify that world freeze must precede disclosure.

Section 7, line 106, provides `HOLD_SOURCE_WORLD_INCOMPLETE` when source-family sufficiency is inadequate. The fictional case instead has substantively adequate local evidence with world-level independent acceptance pending. Pending acceptance is not equivalent to a demonstrated incomplete source family.&#x20;

The existing rules therefore do not unambiguously distinguish:

- Internal authorization of a reviewed atomic component for inclusion in a future world.
- Final authorization to expose that component to the neutral primary task author.

This is material because an early partial-world disclosure could defeat the intended frozen-author-world boundary, even though the individually released original spans are safe and accurately qualified.

It does not invalidate the legitimate unchallenged `PROPOSED / CONDITIONALLY_CLOSED / CHALLENGE_PENDING / RELEASE_HOLD` paths demonstrated in M1 and M2.

### 4.5 Single method-text correction recommended

Insert the following normative qualification into Section 1's precedence rule, and make Section 9's release transition subject to it:

> Atomic-bundle authorization versus task-author disclosure: Independent acceptance and authorization of an exact conditional bundle qualify that bundle for inclusion in a prospective author world; they do not, by themselves, authorize disclosure to the neutral primary task author. Such disclosure is prohibited until the complete exact assembled author-world packet has independently satisfied its required source-family, context, answerability and disclosure gates and has reached `WORLD_FROZEN`. Qualified reviewer-only rendering or boundary testing before world freeze may occur only under its separately authorized protected-access scope and does not constitute primary task-author release. Source-level candidacy, `CONDITIONALLY_CLOSED`, challenge outcomes and substantive HOLD/prohibition rules remain unchanged.

Counter-risk: If applied to all inspection and rendering, this amendment could create another deadlock by preventing the independent author-view testing needed before freeze. The reviewer-only testing exception is therefore essential. It must not become a route for disclosing content to the primary task author.

This correction targets one precise stage-order issue. It does not require rewriting the M1/M2 semantic proposal rules.
