## 2. Six controlled candidate disposition definitions

**The classification describes proposed downstream source treatment, not an admission or a scientific score.** All classifications may be accompanied by unresolved uncertainty; a candidate disposition cannot override an independent author-world construction gate.

| Disposition | Precise permissible meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `CANDIDATE_FULL` | The exact *complete original* Git source blob might be included later in a neutral author world after a real evidence-cited review of content, its time/scope/authority, complete context and contamination. Requires subsequent independent admission and metadata disclosure checks. |
| `CANDIDATE_EXCERPT` | An exact original, reproducible source span might later be included when a larger source is mixed. The review must establish all controlling qualifiers, negations, exceptions, conflicts, supersession and necessary original context, and must verify the excerpt and its metadata do not disclose protected answers or misleading authority. Never fabricate neutral paraphrases as if they were raw source. |
| `AUXILIARY_EVIDENCE_ONLY` | May help an authorized source-status or audit reviewer understand historical facts and disputes, but **may not** be provided to the fresh task author, used to covertly repair author-visible task closure or promoted to governing truth. |
| `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD` | Contains independently identified prohibited old answer/relevance membership, actual R0-P03 task/design/rubric/comparator expectations or other direct content whose disclosure would contaminate a neutral author's world. The evidence must be content-grounded rather than inferred only from a filename or tested keyword; public reports cannot reveal the excluded answer by listing metadata. |
| `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW` | Source role, present status, temporal applicability, contamination, excerpt completeness or other necessary context remains unresolved. An uninspected or truncated source stays on HOLD, not safely allowed or irreversibly irrelevant. |
| `UNSUPPORTED_REPRESENTATION` | Source modality, encoding or faithful interpretation was not available. Do not infer its content or irrelevance. Preserve an exact manifest identity and request appropriate bounded inspection or report source-world incompleteness. |

**Primary versus partial dispositions:** A whole file with inseparable mixed useful and contaminated material may merit `AUXILIARY_EVIDENCE_ONLY` or `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD` for full-file release while carrying a distinct unapproved `CANDIDATE_EXCERPT` proposal for specific exact spans. Such scopes must be separately bound and challenged. No source gets a contradictory implicit “safe full file” because one small excerpt appeared harmless.

## 3. Per-record evidence schema candidate

The following are **semantic requirements**, not a currently implemented machine-readable production schema. Unknown observations must be explicit rather than silently omitted:

- **Identity:** historical source commit, canonical path, Git blob OID, mode, size, SHA-256, supported representation; original exact line/range identity where any quotation or excerpt is proposed.
- **Delivery and inspection:** expected manifest roster entry; actual received bytes and digest; `NOT_RECEIVED`, `PARTIAL_OR_TRUNCATED`, `METADATA_ONLY`, `FULL_RELEVANT_CONTENT_INSPECTED` or `UNSUPPORTED`; reviewer session and batch ID; any source split or resumption. A complete hash/manifest does not itself prove semantic inspection.
- **Assertion as evidence:** exact original claim, claim scope, owner and source role (Product/Project; proposal, accepted decision, normative contract, implementation, test source, observed test, derived projection or other with evidence); distinguish author intent from realized behavior and a tested assertion from actual observed PASS.
- **Temporal scope:** source-claim date, possible effective interval, historical content revision/pin, requested assessment as-of and governing authority hierarchy. Explicitly preserve `UNKNOWN` where no warranted date or precedence exists.
- **Context dependencies:** required preceding/following qualifiers, negative statements, conflicts, succession/exception clauses, external references, incomplete evidence and any minimal context that would change the meaning of the quoted excerpt.
- **Contamination:** `CONFIRMED_DESIGN_OR_GOLD`, `POSSIBLE_SEMANTIC_LEAKAGE`, `NONE_OBSERVED_WITH_REVIEW_SCOPE`, `UNKNOWN`; precise evidence stays only in restricted reviewer output where permitted. “None observed” is not a universal absence proof.
- **Decision and challenge:** record-level disposition, separate exact-span disposition when applicable, reviewer uncertainty, evidence depth, reviewer rationale, candidate-only release status, independent challenge status and any contradictory evidence. Unchallenged, ambiguous mixed decisions cannot be declared admitted.
- **Output destination:** permitted detailed reviewer-only ledger, permitted public aggregate receipt and permitted later exact author-world subset must remain separate, with independently tested release gates.

The reviewer's data should not carry an unqualified assertion such as “this is the correct answer.” A real task's reference entailment is a different later independently adjudicated role, not accomplished merely by classifying a source as author-visible.

## 4. False-positive and false-negative failure testing

Test for both unsafe admission and unjustified exclusion. Minimum synthetic contrast obligations retain V0.1's ten fictional cases, but **the unfinished enum contract** may be calibrated independently without repeating all ten full reports:

1. Logical successor selected but cutover absent: do not turn a proposal/selection into operational truth; retain legitimate historical evidence.
2. Historical state effective at a bounded time: do not substitute review date or projected live state for requested as-of.
3. Code intent and test-source assertion without run receipt: no implementation compliance or PASS inference.
4. Stale derived projection versus newer accepted source: preserve derivation origin and currentness uncertainty.
5. Explicit synthetic expected-answer note: prohibit release to neutral author; restricted audit information may still be necessary.
6. Mixed source with a negating qualifier and comparator prediction: full file and excerpt require **separate** release decisions; the excerpt alone is not safe if controlling context omitted.
7. Unflagged semantic preselected winner: lexical-negative result is not a neutrality certificate.
8. Unsupported binary: neither classify unseen content nor suppress its possible evidentiary need.
9. Missing/truncated batch: no “reviewed all” assertion merely from a complete manifest.
10. Spoiler-bearing filename or exclusion rationale: metadata can itself reveal an answer despite body exclusion.

A calibration must explicitly use the six **defined** disposition tokens where the evidence supports them, or state `UNKNOWN/NOT_CLASSIFIABLE` and why. Mere passing of synthetic tokens is not evidence that the real 2,539-source population was reviewed.

## 5. Access, custody and no-spoiler publication controls

A real reviewer needs its own **actual qualified reviewer-only output destination** before receiving potentially contaminated documents. The destination must preserve complete protected reviewer rationale/paths/spans with exact hashes and verified later recovery, allow controlled resumption and challenge, and be unavailable to the fresh task author, arm authors and unqualified scorers. A normal public Git commit, chat response, raw conversation attachment or an unexamined private ADS companion does not qualify that destination by default.

The public status record, if approved at all, is aggregate and nonanswer-bearing: method/pin commitments, family and inspection counts, holds, unsupported counts, actual qualification status, unambiguous non-secrets version evidence and limited safe reasons. Prohibited are reviewer-private excerpts, raw answer-bearing filenames, exclusion explanations that reveal correctness and joinable mappings from public IDs into private relevance lists.

The eventual neutral author source window is separately built from exact Git blobs/excerpts after source-status review, challenge and metadata-leakage testing. It contains no private ledger annotations, gold, expected answer or comparator hints. Its omissions cannot silently deprive valid task closure; if the remaining source world is incomplete, return `HOLD_SOURCE_WORLD_INCOMPLETE` rather than inventing easier tasks.
