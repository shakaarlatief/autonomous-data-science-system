## 1. Operational meaning of a qualified actor

For any proposed reviewer or challenger, distinguish **three** properties:

1. **Normative role assignment:** a precise, owner-approved reviewer-only or challenger-only job, explicit disqualification from later blind task-author, reference/gold, B0–B4 arm-author and scored-evaluator positions, and assignment of who can challenge/authorize output. A model saying it agrees is *not* a binding independent role assignment.
2. **Observed information-exposure boundary:** what project instructions, prior conversation context, files, memories, connected services and model tools are actually exposed at the selected session/route. The actor must identify tested access, visible-but-unused access, unknown access, external model/provider limits, and earlier relevant project exposure. A new chat title, incognito/Temporary label, clean visible history or separate-model branding is insufficient proof of no latent training or service-side exposure.
3. **Enforceable purpose-specific output route:** a complete, recoverable restricted reviewer/challenger record, under an explicitly selected owner/private custodian and without an ordinary coordinator/author/public-Git access path. Declarations and digest-only public receipts are not equivalent to owner readback or negative-access proof.

The owner may perform file transport and custody, without thereby becoming an independent semantic reviewer or challenger. The exposed coordinator may audit *public-safe* process receipts but cannot challenge its own hidden answer knowledge as if unexposed. Reviewer and challenger cannot be one actor merely using two prompts without separately justified exposure/role independence.

## 3. Admission state machine and hard stops

Use an explicit provisional **A2 evidence state** per gate, with no fake completion through default values:

```text
NOT_TESTED        no qualifying direct evidence yet
EVIDENCE_PENDING  exact test and observation channel are defined, not executed
REPORTED_ONLY     owner or separate session attests it; independent verification incomplete
VERIFIED_BOUNDED  qualifying evidence supports exactly the declared mechanism/scope
FAILED_OR_HOLD    observed failure, missing essential capability, unresolvable uncertainty
```

All gates remain **NOT_TESTED** for the *actual real-source reviewer route* at authoring time, except source-free precedent references in Research 594 and 603, which **are not** transferred to real-route `VERIFIED_BOUNDED`. Do not grant `ADMITTED` because an actor completed an unrelated method-text critique.

For a selected route, `A2_REVIEWER_ADMITTED`, `A2_CHALLENGER_ADMITTED`, `A2_RESTRICTED_CUSTODY_ADMITTED` and `A2_AUTHOR_EXCLUSION_ADMITTED` are separate decisions. A real batch delivery needs all relevant approvals and a new **explicit owner-scoped authorization** for that batch, full manifest and secure destination. Missing owner approval, source access fidelity, material unknown exposed context, protected output, challenger independence or author exclusion **holds** real-source transfer. No hidden experiment role can be reassigned opportunistically.

What cannot be known about provider-internal training, retained process state or account-wide hidden context is documented as **unknown**. The gate must assess whether that unknown can materially affect the actual independence claim; it cannot assert a mathematical zero-exposure guarantee. Where a high-value independence requirement remains unsupported, compare stronger route options (separate provider/account/principal, approved isolated runtime, purpose-specific storage) without assuming any one implementation is mandatory or sufficient.
