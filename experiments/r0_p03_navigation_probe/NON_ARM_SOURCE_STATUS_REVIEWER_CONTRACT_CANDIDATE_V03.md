## 2. Five independent descriptors, one primary decision per release scope

A source path must not be given a single unconditional classification. Record five independent categories of evidence:

1. **Delivery/inspection:** immutable expected source identity; actual received bytes, hash and representation; exact components inspected; access/render fidelity; missing/truncated/unsupported sections; reviewer session and batch identity. Digest equality verifies bytes, **not semantic inspection**.
2. **Epistemic assertion:** exact claim and what it warrants, source's Product/Project owner, historical role and authority, temporal bounds, conflict/counterevidence, and explicit unknown status. A source may establish the existence of a proposal, observation or test assertion without establishing an operational selection, realized implementation or observed PASS.
3. **Contamination and uncertainties:** independently evidenced protected design/gold/comparator content, possible semantic leakage, not observed in a precisely bounded inspected surface, or unknown; each unresolved matter identifies the claim and proposed release **materially** affected.
4. **Requested disclosure scope:** `COMPLETE_FILE`, `EXACT_SPAN`, `REPRESENTATION_COMPONENT`, `AUTHOR_VISIBLE_METADATA`, or `ASSEMBLED_AUTHOR_PACKET` (or reviewer-only audit for which no author release is implied). A different scope has a **different decision** and context dependencies.
5. **Disposition and release state:** one primary proposed disposition from the six tokens below *for the specified scope*, alongside independent reviewer-only utility, rationale, challenge status and explicit `PROPOSED`, `CHALLENGED`, `AUTHORIZED` or `INVALIDATED` state. A proposal or challenge is not automatically final author admission.

The six tokens are **typed proposals for a scope**, not six mutually exclusive descriptions of everything a file represents. The entire file, an excerpt within it, an unreadable diagram, a filename and the final packet can receive different decisions. Never infer component safety from parent prohibition, nor parent safety from one apparently safe component.

## 3. Six candidate dispositions and intended semantics

| Scoped primary token | Operational meaning and required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| `CANDIDATE_FULL` | A *COMPLETE_FILE* may potentially be shown as the **exact original** Git source. The **entire proposed disclosure surface** must have been enumerated and inspected with faithful handling of embedded/linked content and exposed metadata, plus material cross-source qualifications affecting its intended interpretation. No material contamination or context uncertainty can remain. This is still not admission. |
| `CANDIDATE_EXCERPT` | An *EXACT_SPAN* of original source may be proposed even from a mixed or otherwise lengthy file, but only when its exact source, qualifiers, required off-span/off-file context, temporal/authority limits and author-visible metadata are accounted for. Every **material** context dependency must be available in the independently permitted author-world sources; reviewer-only knowledge cannot repair the excerpt. This is still not admission. |
| `AUXILIARY_EVIDENCE_ONLY` | A specifically justified scope is reserved to a permitted reviewer/auditor and **not** shown to the fresh author. Its exclusion reason must be explicit and independent of whether the source is normative. A historically meaningful non-governing proposal, test assertion or observation can still be a legitimate author candidate when otherwise safe; nonnormative is **not** a default auxiliary-only rule. Reviewer utility of prohibited content is tracked independently without overriding prohibition. |
| `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD` | **Confirmed**, evidence-grounded protected expected answers, relevance membership, old task/design/rubric or comparator material makes the **affected exposure scope** prohibited for a neutral author. Reviewer/audit value remains a separate attribute. Suspicion without sufficient confirmation causes scoped HOLD instead. An unsafe whole file does not automatically prohibit all separately challenged genuine source spans. |
| `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW` | Missing or truncated bytes, material unknown/possible contamination, missing controlling context, disputed decisive authority/effective time, unresolved review coverage or missing release authorization blocks the **affected proposed scope**. A missing *source* receives HOLD with `NOT_RECEIVED`, not unsupported-format classification. Nonmaterial uncertainty for one narrower historic claim need not block an unrelated properly supported claim. |
| `UNSUPPORTED_REPRESENTATION` | Specific **received** bytes cannot be faithfully interpreted/rendered in the declared representation. Apply to the affected representation component; dependent file/excerpt/packet scopes remain HOLD if its unknown content can materially control their meaning or contamination. Do not infer semantic irrelevance from decoding failure. |

No candidate token itself upgrades source authority, independent factual correctness, hidden reference entailment, or eventual author packet. A confirmed prohibited component blocks **any proposed exposure containing it**, regardless of proposed reviewer utility. If separate, an excerpt still needs its own verified context closure and metadata challenge.

## 4. Prospective decision procedure for each proposed scope

This is an explicit **logical decision discipline**, not an automated model claiming to judge semantic truth.

**Step A. Bind identity and proposed use.** Record source commit/path/blob/hash/mode, full or exact-span/metadata/packet scope and **what claim and audience** the exposure is meant to support. Preserve original evidence lines separately from interpretive annotations.

**Step B. Establish received bytes, faithful interpretation and inspection coverage.** If expected bytes are absent/partial, scope is HOLD. If bytes exist but a necessary representation is unreadable, mark the component UNSUPPORTED and every materially dependent proposed exposure HOLD. The entire complete-file exposure cannot receive `CANDIDATE_FULL` on a `FULL_RELEVANT_CONTENT_INSPECTED` flag; it needs **COMPLETE_DISCLOSURE_SURFACE_INSPECTED**, including appendices, associated files disclosed in the same artifact, embedded assets, metadata and every relevant part actually presented. A source blob being hashed does not prove this flag.

**Step C. Evaluate evidence role, time and conflicts.** Distinguish record creation/publication, claim-as-of, proposed effective change, independently evidenced realized transition/effective interval, observed implementation/test result and requested assessment-as-of. No recency-based automatic hierarchy, no inferred activation from a selected target, and no inferred continuing authority from lack of rollback. Preserve unresolved conflict evidence instead of overwriting it.

**Step D. Test disclosure-specific contamination and material uncertainty.** Mark which exact scope/claim a protected cue, ambiguity, unsupported representation or missing context would affect. Confirmed design/gold within the disclosed material: PROHIBIT for that scope. Material possible leakage, unsupported context or decisive unknown: HOLD. Nonmaterial unknowns are reported, but do not automatically prohibit a separately bounded supported historical assertion.

**Step E. Inspect permission and context closure.** Where a proposed candidate can be accurately interpreted with all materially required *independently permitted author-visible original evidence*, and its proposed disclosed metadata carries no prohibited inference, assign the relevant candidate token; otherwise HOLD. Reviewer-only annotations, neutral-sounding invented paraphrases or an omitted protected governing qualifier cannot silently supply an explanatory dependency.

**Step F. Independently challenge and authorize, separately.** A candidate disposition is at most `PROPOSED`. The separately qualified challenger may contest evidence, completeness, context, uncertainty, status, leaks and false exclusions; preserve original decisions and disagreements. Only later source-world construction/admission can mark an author-visible scope `AUTHORIZED`, after packet-level collection and version-diff checks. Missing challenge, incomplete custody or scope-specific gate failure prevents authorization.

**Step G. Invalidate dependent proposals when evidence changes.** A revised role/status verdict, contaminated filename, source-manifest identity, excerpt qualifier or temporal dependency must reopen every affected downstream excerpt, metadata and packet decision. Do not overwrite prior rejected or challenged verdicts and pretend the history was always clean.

### Precedence for a fixed exposure scope

Apply guards in a fixed evidence order, while maintaining the **independent component and reviewer-utility fields**:

| Evidence condition for this release scope | Controlling status |
| --- | --- |
| Expected source not received or incomplete | `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW` with delivery deficit |
| Necessary delivered representation cannot be inspected | `UNSUPPORTED_REPRESENTATION` for component; dependent release scopes `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW` |
| Confirmed protected content would be exposed | `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD` |
| Potential protected leakage, required context or temporal applicability remains materially unresolved | `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW` |
| Reviewer/audit retention needed, and a separately justified author-exclusion rule applies | `AUXILIARY_EVIDENCE_ONLY`, never a synonym for “not governing” |
| Fully evaluated release surface and author-visible controlling context, no material leaks or unknowns | `CANDIDATE_FULL` for a complete file or `CANDIDATE_EXCERPT` for an exact span; other scopes need their own explicit permit/hold decision |

**Evidence-order nuance:** This is not permission to hide already confirmed contamination because other source bytes are missing; preserve all confirmed contamination flags separately. The table selects the scoped *release* primary token based on the currently evaluable evidence. Never suppress more restrictive material facts or derive a safe token merely because the first matching condition is absent.

For author-visible **metadata** and an **assembled packet**, these six candidate source labels are not automatically sufficient. Record a dedicated scoped `RELEASE_HOLD` / `CHALLENGE_PENDING` / `AUTHORIZATION_GRANTED` gate, with the applicable source dispositions and complete dependency evidence. Do not invent a seventh source disposition masquerading as one of the six.

## 5. Temporal and context closure witnesses

**Temporal applicability witness** for each material proposed claim contains: (a) source event and authored date if known; (b) claim's actual period/scope; (c) decision date and *proposed* activation if any; (d) separately evidenced actual operational change and supersession if present; (e) requested evaluation-as-of; (f) authority/conflict precedence evidence or `UNKNOWN`; (g) exact source coordinates and unresolved limits. A historical statement may remain supportable even if its later operation is unproven.

**Context dependency witness** contains: source blob and exact line/span/component reference; nature of controlling negation, exception, changed role, supersession, external contradiction or metadata implication; scope of affected claim; whether the full necessary qualifying context is accessible in the **candidate author-visible world** without private spoiler disclosure; and the reviewer/challenger decision. Every **material** unresolved edge blocks the dependent excerpt/packet. Edges must not silently disappear when a later review changes the parent.

**Whole-file review witness** separately inventories complete proposed bytes, every actually exposed line/section/appendix, embedded formats, link/attachment disclosures and author-visible path/title/label metadata; what was and was not semantically inspected and who verified it. If a material portion is uninspected, `CANDIDATE_FULL` is not supported.

These witnesses are still **reviewer proposals** until independently challenged, and must be preserved only in qualified reviewer-only custody once they contain real protected contents.

## 6. Multi-source and historical-publication disclosure checks

Each packet is a separate disclosure subject. Source paths, captions, aliases, order, omissions, line numbers, exact extracted quotes, cross-links, joinable identifiers, exclusions and prior aggregate receipts can jointly reveal protected answer expectations or a misleading unqualified authority. Compare the **assembled author packet** and relevant observable public publication history as a unit, not merely each individual source.

Private provenance must preserve the true Git path, blob identity and exact bytes. A neutral author-facing display alias, if permitted and independently justified, may hide unsafe names without claiming that an alias is original source metadata. The policy must test whether a repeated public receipt or joins across two published reports reveal the protected mapping.

If a packet cannot preserve all material conflicts/exceptions/effective-date evidence inside the **permitted author-visible source world**, return `HOLD_SOURCE_WORLD_INCOMPLETE`. Do not repair missing primary-source context with reviewer notes, invent independent “neutral” explanations, omit the hard category, or choose easier scored questions.

A reviewer’s restricted decision ledger may contain dangerous source-specific context. It must remain separate from (a) a content-safe public progress receipt and (b) a later frozen author-source packet. The latter requires additional independent authorization, not a whitelist produced by a lexical or metadata-only scan.

## 7. Append-only challenge, revision and invalidation

A candidate reviewer verdict record should be version-bound to the immutable source, stated claim and explicit release scope, include reviewer identity/exposure/inspection evidence, material uncertainties, relevant original source coordinates and context dependencies. A challenger must be a **separately authorized reviewer**, not a new label on an exposed coordinator.

Changes append superseding disposition entries with explicit reason and linkage, retaining prior challenge/adjudication decisions. The set of affected dependent spans and packets must be recomputed before any claimed re-authorization. The technical representation of this ledger, confidentiality, resumption and verification are **not yet implemented**; do not describe the semantic schema as a working protected store.

A safe public progress receipt includes only independently checked, aggregate method, pin, family, coverage, holds and limitations, and is evaluated for public-history differencing. No raw reviewer-private filenames, excluded gold text, case-specific old relevance keys or cross-linkable disposal reasons may be published.
