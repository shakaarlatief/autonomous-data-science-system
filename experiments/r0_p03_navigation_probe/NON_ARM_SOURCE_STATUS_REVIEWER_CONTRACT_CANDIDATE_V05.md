## 1. Status model: eligibility is never authority to disclose

Do **not** conflate these four separate decisions:

1. **Source proposition:** What a pinned source supports for a precisely stated claim, time and applicability domain, with explicit counterevidence and uncertainty.
2. **Semantic and disclosure candidacy:** Whether one exact source blob or excerpt could *potentially* be disclosed, given fully examined exposure, risks, provenance and any declared co-release dependencies.
3. **Independent challenge and authorization:** Whether a separately qualified reviewer/challenger and authorized release authority have actually validated an exact, bounded exposure and its rendered interface, with protected readback evidence and no unresolved material objection.
4. **Whole author-world adequacy:** Whether the final frozen packet, as a collection, contains sufficient permitted original source context and required families without hidden answer repair, spoilers or deceptive omissions.

The six historical candidate disposition names operate only in **Step 2 for source content proposals**. They do not mean `AUTHORIZED`, and missing external challenge, unqualified custody or missing release permission **never** changes an otherwise supported `CANDIDATE_FULL / PROPOSED` into `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW`. Those deficiencies block the separate **exposure gate**.

**Proposal-stage context qualification versus release-stage acceptance (normative precedence):** At `PROPOSED`, a source-content candidate may be recorded if all *substantive* evidence, exact-source inspection, claim/context dependencies, exposure screening, and bounded negative-search adequacy needed for its scoped proposition are sufficiently established. A complete, substantively inspected dependency bundle may be recorded as `CONDITIONALLY_CLOSED` **only as a typed dependency-closure attribute**, never as an additional source disposition, independent challenge result, source release, or final author-world acceptance. A substantively adequate but not yet independently challenged evidence argument retains its eligible semantic candidate, with `CHALLENGE_PENDING` and separate `RELEASE_HOLD`; missing challenge, custody or authorization is not itself a semantic defect. A missing, prohibited, unsupported, incompletely inspected or materially uncertain context member, or a materially inadequate coverage witness, **still imposes the appropriate scoped semantic HOLD/prohibition**. The positive-context dependencies and material negative-coverage limits must be independently accepted, and the exact assembled exposure independently authorized, **before any author disclosure or world freeze**. This precedence governs the corresponding wording in Sections 3, 5, 7 and 9.

No actor is assigned a real reviewer, challenger or author role by producing a synthetic report. A Temporary Chat, exact bytes or new conversation cannot alone prove independence, confidential storage or source access.

## 2. Typed subjects and permitted decision values

A review record has an immutable subject type. Do not label all subjects with a single universal disposition.

| Subject type | Primary source candidate/disposition domain | Independent outcome/gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `COMPLETE_FILE` | `CANDIDATE_FULL`, `AUXILIARY_EVIDENCE_ONLY`, `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD`, `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW` | `PROPOSED`, `CHALLENGE_PENDING`, `CHALLENGED_ACCEPTED`, `CHALLENGED_REJECTED`, `CHALLENGED_DISPUTED`, `INVALIDATED`; release gate separate |
| `EXACT_SPAN` | `CANDIDATE_EXCERPT`, `AUXILIARY_EVIDENCE_ONLY`, `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD`, `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW` | Same proposal/challenge states; admission separate |
| `REPRESENTATION_COMPONENT` | `UNSUPPORTED_REPRESENTATION`, `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD`, `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW`; or `SUPPORTED_INSPECTED` **as an inspection result, not seventh disposition** | Component evidence does not authorize its containing file/span |
| `AUTHOR_VISIBLE_METADATA` | No source-content six-token disposition; use `ELIGIBLE_PROPOSED`, `RELEASE_HOLD` or `PROHIBITED_EXPOSURE` as typed disclosure-screen results | Independent rendered author-interface and cumulative-leakage challenge |
| `ASSEMBLED_AUTHOR_PACKET` or `CONDITIONAL_BUNDLE` | No source-content six-token disposition; use `ELIGIBLE_PROPOSED`, `RELEASE_HOLD` or `PROHIBITED_EXPOSURE` as packet-screen results | Independent exact packet/version authorization; author-world sufficiency additional |
| `REVIEWER_ONLY_AUDIT` | No source author-disposition by itself; record reviewer-evidence utility separately | Requires verified confidential output authorization, without author release |

`HOLD_SOURCE_WORLD_INCOMPLETE` is a **reason code for `RELEASE_HOLD` at packet/world scope**, not a seventh source disposition. `NOT_SELECTED_FOR_PACKET` is a **packet-curation outcome**, not `AUXILIARY_EVIDENCE_ONLY`, negative evidence about a source, or a source safety verdict.

A readable component's `SUPPORTED_INSPECTED` indicates only representation and inspection sufficiency for the declared component. Eligibility for a complete file or original exact span still requires content-level classification; metadata and assembled packets require their own scoped release screens. A prohibited full blob may contain a separately assessed clean excerpt without contradiction. No child proposal overrides a parent/assembled exposure veto.

## 3. Exactly six source-content disposition meanings

- `CANDIDATE_FULL`: The **entire proposed author-facing complete original file disclosure surface**, including body, relevant embedded assets, actually loaded linked assets and any exposed metadata, has been inventoried and faithfully inspected. Proposed claims and their bounded context have no material truth or disclosure uncertainty; no confirmed prohibited material would be exposed. No downstream permission is implied.
- `CANDIDATE_EXCERPT`: One pinned **original exact byte/line span** is a possible author source, with all governing qualifications and temporal/context dependencies identified. Co-release dependencies can be `CONDITIONALLY_CLOSED` as a **typed dependency-closure attribute** bound to an exact proposed atomic-bundle manifest, independently of the primary six-token source-content disposition; this attribute does not imply already accepted or authorized context. Excerpt plus permitted author-visible original context and metadata must be sufficient at eventual release. An otherwise clean large file can qualify for excerpt-only exposure.
- `AUXILIARY_EVIDENCE_ONLY`: Source is useful for a qualified reviewer-only audit and **an explicitly governing author-exclusion policy** prevents disclosure of that scope, independent of a source being nonnormative or inconvenient to an intended packet. Record the approved policy identifier and its source/applicability. If no such policy can be evidenced, do not fabricate the reason: hold the unresolved scope, or retain genuine candidacy where it meets eligibility conditions. Confirmed design/gold contamination must instead be `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD` for its affected exposure.
- `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD`: Confirmed protected expected-answer, old relevance, task, rubric or comparator content makes the **specified exposed content** unacceptable for a neutral author. Preserve exact restricted evidence and a separate reviewer-utility flag where permitted. Mere suspicion does not prove prohibition but materially possible leakage still causes HOLD. This prohibition is a **persistent release veto for every exposure guaranteed to include the confirmed material**.
- `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW`: The proposed source use has a material unresolved source-delivery, inspection, context, authority, contamination or semantic evidence dependency. Record precisely which claim, exposure and remedial evidence are missing. An unrelated future deployment uncertainty need not block a well-supported past proposal claim. **Missing release authorization alone is never a semantic HOLD reason.**
- `UNSUPPORTED_REPRESENTATION`: Faithfully rendering or interpreting **received** bytes of a particular representation component is unavailable. Do not apply this token to expected but missing bytes (delivery HOLD). The unreadable component and any semantically dependent file/excerpt/packet are separate subjects; the latter remain on HOLD unless proven independent of the component.

These six names are retained for compatibility with Research 592–595. They are not automatically a mutually exclusive ontology of source usefulness, epistemic role, reviewer utility, risk, or final release.

## 4. Dual materiality and explicit compound-blocker semantics

Every unresolved item needs **two independent questions**:

1. **Claim materiality:** If the fact resolved adversely, could it change the truth, scope, historical authority, effective time, or the claim the author might reasonably infer?
2. **Disclosure materiality:** If exposed or inferred through the specific content, linked asset, viewer, metadata, packet or public-history join, could the fact leak protected design/gold or materially mislead the author **even when irrelevant to the intended factual claim**?

Record the exact uncertain proposition, evidence, affected claim and exposure, plausible adverse outcome, reason for materiality or independently supported nonmateriality, inspection scope and challenge status. `NONE_OBSERVED_WITH_REVIEW_SCOPE` is not proof of global absence. Where an adverse consequential interpretation cannot be ruled out, the affected exposure remains blocked. Preserve narrowly supported historical facts rather than inventing a blanket ban.

**Compound blocker rule, per exact proposed exposure:**

- Preserve all orthogonal defects as independent **blocking reasons**: missing bytes, unsupported component, unresolved authority/claim, possible leakage, confirmed protected content, unqualified custody or challenge.
- If confirmed protected content is **guaranteed** to be included in the proposed exposure, it imposes an unconditional `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD` **source-content primary label/veto** for a file/span, irrespective of other missing sections. For metadata or packet, the typed screen is `PROHIBITED_EXPOSURE`. Missing delivery is separately recorded and cannot cure the prohibition.
- If no such confirmed veto and material content is missing, the file/span has `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW` with delivery reason. A received but unreadable representation has `UNSUPPORTED_REPRESENTATION` at the component, and materially dependent parent exposure HOLD.
- If neither veto nor missing source applies but material possible leakage, temporal conflict, off-scope exception, or incomplete semantic inspection persists, the file/span remains HOLD.
- If complete scoped evidence supports author visibility and the source has no material blockers, it can be `CANDIDATE_FULL` or `CANDIDATE_EXCERPT`, even when release/challenge/custody gates are not yet satisfied; those gates remain `RELEASE_HOLD` for the exposure.
- If an accepted, independently evidenced **author-exclusion policy** legitimately applies despite reviewer utility and no confirmed design/gold veto, `AUXILIARY_EVIDENCE_ONLY` may apply. No author-exclusion policy is invented merely because a safe record was not selected.
- For a composite incomplete exposure, preserve `PARTIAL_INFORMATION` and **do not claim absence** of prohibited material in missing components. Known vetoes persist across delivery repairs and require explicit scoped evidence to invalidate.

No silent single-label rewriting is allowed when new bytes arrive; new evidence creates an append-only revision and recomputes any dependent proposal and packet.

## 5. Content exposure closure, loading and bounded links

A full-file hash identifies its Git bytes, **not** automatically loaded assets, evolving web targets, rendered viewer metadata or future behavior. Classify all references as:

- `ACTUALLY_EMBEDDED_OR_LOADED`: inspect, hash and freeze exact loaded bytes and rendering; if unfrozen dynamic loads remain, release is held. Redirects, transitive loads, alternate renderings and media generated by the viewer must be included in the bounded exposure policy.
- `NAVIGABLE_AUTHOR_ACCESS`: if author action can open it, it is a possible disclosure and requires a qualified allowlist, isolated snapshot or confirmed disabled navigation; it is not automatically inert.
- `MATERIAL_CONTEXT_DEPENDENCY`: necessary to interpret a claim or exception, even if technically unlinked. At **semantic proposal stage**, sufficiently identify, deliver and faithfully inspect the necessary original context, its governing qualifications, and its dependency edges. A supported source proposal may reference an exact, substantively assessed **proposed** `CONDITIONAL_BUNDLE` with `CONDITIONALLY_CLOSED` as a dependency attribute; independent acceptance is not required **to record** that proposal. If necessary context is absent, uninspected, prohibited or materially inadequate, assign the appropriate scoped substantive HOLD/veto instead. At **author disclosure stage**, the required original context must be independently accepted and released as a permitted atomic author-visible bundle under the separate release gate; otherwise preserve `RELEASE_HOLD` on the exposure. Never use reviewer-private explanation to replace missing original context.
- `INERT_CITATION`: reference not loaded, not navigable and independently demonstrated immaterial for the precisely bounded claim. Its inaccessibility alone does **not** force an otherwise valid narrow historical observation to HOLD.

A link may carry multiple tags. The policy must avoid an infinite closure demand for every incidental URL while rejecting a false PASS where dynamic remote content or a viewer-generated filename changes what the author sees.

The **author-view witness** must bind exact manifest, permitted bytes, content-rendering path, transformations, linked assets, citation/download/search/hover/preview fields, path aliases and redirects. A neutral filename alias is only a display treatment; private authentic Git identity remains exact and separately recoverable. If the actual author surface is untested or differs materially from the frozen witness, `RELEASE_HOLD`.

## 6. Claim time, bounded applicability and conflict

Record authoring date, claim-as-of, proposed decision/activation, evidence of actual activation and observed behavior, valid-time interval, record-time provenance, supersession, as-of requested, and applicability **domain** (environment/site/population/version/owner/scope). One site's observed cutover does not justify a universal “active everywhere” header.

Where hierarchy or continuity is contested, preserve the incompatible claims and source provenance. Later date and path/name alone do not prove superior authority; no rollback note does not prove permanent activation. A supportable historical statement such as “option was proposed on date X” remains eligible even if later actual deployment is unknown. The stronger operational claim stays HOLD.

## 7. Context-search and conditional atomic bundle closure

Every context-completeness proposal needs a **bounded negative-search/coverage witness**, not merely “no conflict found”:

- exact frozen source-universe manifest, expected population/strata, candidate and excluded families, source pin and unsupported formats;
- inspected semantic depth per record/family, explicit search strategy for governing exceptions and effective-status conflicts, alternative representations and unreviewed/partial gaps;
- known controlling off-span/off-file dependencies, conflicting claims and review-only records that may contain missing qualifications;
- a reasoned account of whether still-unreviewed sources could materially alter the proposed claim or author exposure, including substantive evidence, identified limitations, adverse scenarios and **limits identified for subsequent independent challenge**.

This does not demand omniscience or equal full-model attention to all 2,539 records. It does **prohibit** deriving source-world closure solely from keyword-negative scans, a partial ledger or the absence of known exceptions.

**Typed coverage precedence:** A bounded negative-search witness must be *substantively adequate* for the precisely proposed claim even before independent challenge: it identifies a frozen population, actual inspection depth, material exceptions/dependencies and the evidenced limits on what unreviewed strata might change. If a material gap, plausible controlling exception, unsupported coverage or uninspected dependency remains unresolved, the **affected source-content file/span** receives `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW` with the precise deficient claim/exposure and a separate packet/world `RELEASE_HOLD` when relevant. If substantive negative-search adequacy is established but the independent challenge has not happened, preserve the supported source disposition at `PROPOSED`, record `CHALLENGE_PENDING`, and hold the **exposure** at `RELEASE_HOLD`; do **not** relabel the semantic source as HOLD solely for missing independent challenge. Independent acceptance of the bounded coverage witness is required **before release** of the corresponding exposure and before world freeze. Inadequate source-family sufficiency at packet/world scope uses `RELEASE_HOLD` reason `HOLD_SOURCE_WORLD_INCOMPLETE`, not a seventh source disposition or an untyped claim-level HOLD.

**Conditional co-release:** Two (or more) exact permitted source proposals may be mutually dependent. Represent their dependency as a proposed `CONDITIONAL_BUNDLE` with a complete membership manifest and explicit claim/context edges. A semantically supported source candidate can carry the **distinct** `CONDITIONALLY_CLOSED` dependency attribute against that proposed bundle, even when member challenge and authorization are pending. The typed dependency record must identify the exact membership manifest, relevant claims, controlling original text spans, inspection evidence, material negative-coverage witness, and complete context edges; it cannot refer to uninspected placeholders. The primary source disposition and candidate `PROPOSED` status remain separate. **No bundle member is individually or atomically released at proposal stage.** Independently challenge, accept and authorize the exact atomic bundle before disclosure, preventing both circular deadlock and premature individual release. If one component is prohibited, missing or unqualified and necessary to the meaning of the others, the bundle stays held. Dependency cycles are evaluated at bundle scope rather than treated as proof that each separate item is already admitted.

For each proposed `EXACT_SPAN`, list controlling original text dependencies, including negatives, exceptions, authority, time and relevant metadata. A required exception known only from restricted reviewer evidence **cannot** be supplied through reviewer explanation or a fabricated neutral summary. No packet is source-world-sufficient without all material author-visible context, regardless of individual candidate statuses.

## 8. Separate source, packet, world and publication outcomes

- **Source candidacy:** Proposed candidate or scoped HOLD/PROHIBIT/AUXILIARY with evidence and challenge state. A source may be eligible yet `NOT_SELECTED_FOR_PACKET`, with no implication that it is irrelevant or prohibited.
- **Packet membership:** A particular immutable manifest, exact source dependency bundle, original-span selection, displayed aliases, asset snapshots and author-view rendering receives `ELIGIBLE_PROPOSED`, `RELEASE_HOLD` or `PROHIBITED_EXPOSURE` from a separate packet disclosure screen. Pairwise safe items may still combine to leak an answer.
- **World sufficiency:** Beyond packet safety, independently check original research source-family closure, context, evidentiary answerability and the hard original task/reference separation boundaries. `HOLD_SOURCE_WORLD_INCOMPLETE` is a packet/world gate reason, **not** a source disposition.
- **Public receipt:** Each published progress aggregate needs an adversarial join/differencing check against the **entire observable public history** and any known external enumeration. Counts involving potentially isolating strata must be suppressed/coarsened or held. No invented universal minimum cell threshold is claimed here. Public metadata safety is distinct from confidential ledger safety.

No publication of real answer-bearing filenames, excluded member lists, source-to-answer mappings or private reviewer justifications. Historical source identity must remain privately recoverable without making its canonical filename author-visible when the name itself is a spoiler.

## 9. Append-only roles, challenge and release transition scheme

The following are **requested contractual states**, not an implemented private source-review infrastructure:

| Layer | Transition and required evidence | Failure behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Semantic reviewer | `UNREVIEWED` -> `PROPOSED` with exact subject/source, sufficient substantive inspection, claim, dual materiality, coverage witness and any exact `CONDITIONALLY_CLOSED` bundle dependency record | Substantive content/coverage deficit yields scoped HOLD/PROHIBIT; pending challenge alone preserves a supported `PROPOSED` source candidate and keeps the exposure at `RELEASE_HOLD` |
| Independent challenger | `CHALLENGE_PENDING` -> `CHALLENGED_ACCEPTED`, `CHALLENGED_REJECTED`, or `CHALLENGED_DISPUTED` with separately qualified actor and preserved challenge evidence | Unresolved **material** dissent prevents release, not preservation of a semantic candidate proposal |
| Release authorization | `RELEASE_HOLD` -> `AUTHORIZATION_GRANTED` only through a separately approved authority and exact immutable exposure manifest, challenged packet, verified protected custody/readback, author-view rendering and cumulative-public-history checks | Absence of permission maintains HOLD even when semantic `CANDIDATE_FULL` is supported |
| Author-world freeze | `WORLD_UNFROZEN` -> `WORLD_FROZEN` only after permitted original source coverage, four-family answerability capacity, neutral metadata/context and independent acceptance | A packet of individually eligible files does not automatically freeze the world |
| Revisions | Append superseding source/claim/context/metadata records; recompute dependency closure and invalidate authorizations/packets affected by altered inputs | Prior verdicts retained; dependent exposures are unusable until requalified |

Specific reviewer, challenger and authorizer identities, custody implementation, access checks and signed readback evidence are **not qualified**. No unassigned party can mark `AUTHORIZATION_GRANTED` or `WORLD_FROZEN`. A synthetic critic's PASS cannot supply these authorities.

## 10. Independent synthetic exit and engineering progression

A separately supplied **source-free V0.4** reviewer critique (Research 605) found two material ambiguities: M1, the conditional original-context bundle could be required to have completed independent acceptance *before* an eligible proposal; and M2, a substantively adequate bounded negative-search witness could be semantically held merely because its independent challenge was pending. That critic recommended `AMEND_MATERIAL_CONTRADICTION` and did **not** certify V0.4.

This V0.5 coordinator amendment resolves the two identified contradictory decision paths **textually** by separating (i) sufficiently evidenced semantic proposals and typed conditional dependencies, (ii) pending or completed independent challenge, (iii) exact authorized disclosure, and (iv) world adequacy. An unqualified external critic has not yet independently tested **V0.5**, and this author must not certify their own repair.

**Minimum direct follow-up criterion:** If a subsequent independently scoped method check is needed, test exactly two invented situations without reopening the entire old fictional test suite: (a) two fully inspected, permitted, mutually context-dependent original sources forming a complete but unchallenged atomic proposal; and (b) a narrow historical proposition with an evidenced substantively adequate negative-search witness but no challenger yet assigned. Each must yield a supported semantic `PROPOSED` and distinct `CHALLENGE_PENDING / RELEASE_HOLD`. Then vary each case to make one controlling member missing, prohibited, insufficiently inspected or coverage-materially uncertain and require the corresponding substantive HOLD/veto, not automatic candidacy. This is an acceptance criterion, **not a claim those tests have been independently run**.

A further material contradiction or observed implementation failure merits a targeted fix. Editorial suggestions alone do not require another method version or arbitrary repeated Temporary Chat rehearsals.
