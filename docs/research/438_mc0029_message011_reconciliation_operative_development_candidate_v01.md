## 5. RC-3: requirements amendments R-1 through R-7

### R-1 authored-semantics qualification property

**ACCEPT.**

For machine semantics that are explicitly authored and owner/governance accepted, zero-context inter-author agreement is not the primary validity criterion.

Primary properties become:

    faithfulness to accepted intent
    structural / grammar validity
    deterministic consumer behavior
    leak detection
    competency-question sufficiency
    adoption cost

Independent-author reproducibility remains relevant for inferential mechanisms such as:

    legacy candidate extraction
    leak detection
    optional drafting diagnostics
    any future component that still claims to infer semantics from unconstrained text

### R-2 machine-answer scope

**ACCEPT WITH IMPORTANT REFINEMENT.**

The machine is not required to convert all human-governing meaning into operational machine semantics.

The target distinction is:

    HUMAN-GOVERNING MEANING
        may include rationale, principles, design freedoms, interpretation context

    MACHINE-OPERATIVE MEANING
        only meaning deliberately admitted to deterministic machine consumption

Non-operative text is not declared non-authoritative. It may remain authoritative to humans and future governance. It simply does not automatically create a machine control consequence.

The architecture must make this boundary explicit and detect material leakage where feasible.

### R-3 authoring legitimacy

**ACCEPT.**

The architecture must define who may propose machine-operative clauses, who accepts them, what acceptance means, and how owner/governance review quality is protected.

This promotes the previously deferred owner-review efficacy risk to an early viability question for any candidate that concentrates machine semantics at acceptance.

### R-4 expressiveness boundary

**ACCEPT.**

A successor must state what it intentionally refuses to make machine-operative and why.

Failure to structure all human meaning is not itself a defect if consequential machine questions remain answerable and omissions are visible.

### R-5 clause-level lifecycle

**ACCEPT AS DEVELOPMENT CANDIDATE, NOT FINAL ENUM.**

Partial supersession, amendment, retirement and deferral need a granularity smaller than whole-document status where consequential semantics differ by scope.

An addressable operative clause is a promising unit.

The exact clause lifecycle grammar is not frozen until competency questions and small replays show it is sufficient.

### R-6 executable derivation semantics

**ACCEPT AND STRENGTHEN.**

Where a Project-system result is claimed to be deterministic and consequential, the normative machine definition must be executable and regression-tested.

Prose may explain the rule but does not independently define a second algorithm.

This does not mean every semantic concept must become code. It applies to deterministic derived decisions/predicates the architecture claims machines can evaluate.

### R-7 shared primitive specialization

**ACCEPT AS A CANDIDATE SPECIALIZATION, NOT AN IMMEDIATE RENAME.**

DRP-01 empirically supported an obligation_reference shared seam.

Under OPERATIVE, the likely successor is a more concrete reference such as:

    operative_clause_reference
    or
    governing_clause_reference

The exact name and boundary must be requalified against the DRP-01 seam inventory rather than silently rewriting a passed result.

## 11. OPERATIVE V0.1 logical architecture

The current leading development candidate is:

### 11.1 One governed acceptance object

A governing artifact contains:

    human-readable context / rationale / evidence / principles
    machine-operative clause block

Both are accepted in one governing act.

Machine-operative semantics are addressable and parseable.

### 11.2 Minimal closed grammar

Illustrative forms from Message 011 are retained as hypotheses:

    REQUIRE
    PROHIBIT
    GATE
    INVARIANT
    SUPERSEDE
    AMEND
    RETIRE
    DEFER
    ASSIGN

No form is frozen merely because Claude proposed it.

SP-1 must justify every form and slot through named competency questions and consumers.

### 11.3 Realization-time coverage

Realizing artifacts/domains declare coverage over accepted operative clause IDs.

Acceptance-time authors do not predict canonical implementation grouping.

Coverage becomes the leading formulation of KA-R52:

    every active realization-requiring operative clause
        has valid realization coverage
        OR
        has a valid governed deferral / exception according to its clause semantics

The exact universal quantifier and exception model remain development questions.

### 11.4 Executable predicates

Derived operational truth is implemented once in executable semantics with golden tests.

Candidate predicates include:

    coverage_present
    evidence_valid
    qualification_complete
    activation_effective
    deferral_effective
    gate_satisfied
    prohibition_active
    conflict_present

This is illustrative. SP-1/SP-3 determine the minimum set.

### 11.5 LLM role

LLMs may:

    draft clauses
    explain them
    propose legacy candidates
    detect likely recital leaks
    perform diagnostic independent drafts
    suggest realizer coverage

LLM output is not machine-authoritative until admitted through the appropriate governed act or natural-owner fact path.

### 11.6 Human-readable break-glass property

The accepted machine-operative meaning should remain inspectable without requiring a proprietary model or opaque embedding/index.

Generated indexes and projections may accelerate normal operation but cannot be the sole place where governing meaning exists.

## 13. Important refinement: deferral is not syntax alone

Message 011 says deferral validity can become grammar validity.

That is directionally useful but too strong if read literally.

A valid DEFER clause may require mechanically checkable semantics such as:

    authorized governing actor
    exact clause/scope reference
    temporal/revision applicability
    closure/reactivation condition
    required evidence path

Therefore the target is:

    DEFER validity = structural validity + deterministic authority/scope/temporal checks

not merely successful parsing.

The benefit remains: no later semantic author decides whether an arbitrary prose hold should count as a deferral.

## 14. Important refinement: machine-operative does not exhaust human normativity

OPERATIVE must not silently redefine the project's human authority model.

A principle or design freedom may be genuinely governing to humans while deliberately excluded from direct machine control.

Therefore:

    machine-operative block
        = exhaustive for claimed machine consequences

not:

    machine-operative block
        = exhaustive of every normative meaning humans may use

SP-1/SP-2 must test whether this separation supports the actual Project-system competency questions without consequential leakage.
