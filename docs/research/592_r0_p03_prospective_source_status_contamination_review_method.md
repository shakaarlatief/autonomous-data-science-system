## 1. Exact decision to support

The central risk is two-sided: historical sources contain legitimate evidence about what was proposed, accepted, implemented, tested, superseded, recovered and observed, while some also contain Research 217/W5 relevance keys, prior experiment answers or R0-P03 comparator and task designs. Blanket historical exclusion can make genuine whole-system needs unsatisfiable. Blanket admission can invalidate author independence. A lexical flag cannot resolve this trade-off.

The next artifact must be a source-status/contamination **adjudication record**, produced through a qualified non-arm review process, rather than a new larger unexamined source ZIP or another selected task list.

## 2. Actors and exposure boundary

1. **Exposed coordinator:** may define a review schema, deterministic tools, integrity tests, source family coverage expectations and prospective stop criteria. It must not self-certify the 2,133 records as safe, independently select tasks, author hidden references or evaluate scored arms.
2. **Separate source-status reviewer:** reviews content and evidence under a declared role, accessible corpus, tool/context inventory and independence/exposure statement. Because detection of prior experiment design may require inspecting contaminated material, this actor is explicitly **not** eligible later as a fresh primary-task author, independent reference annotator or scored evaluator. Review independence concerns reviewer authorship and adjudication controls, not a fictitious claim that the reviewer never saw hazardous text.
3. **Fresh task author:** sees only the later qualified, frozen source world plus a task-neutral brief, never this adjudication's internal spoiler excerpts or prior old-gold/arm design. Research 579 selects this route over the currently unsupported mechanical historical-event selector.
4. **Independent reference adjudicator and later evaluators:** remain separate, prospectively qualified roles with complete separately protected versioned gold and the existing Research 559/576 requirements.

A reviewer being different from the exposed coordinator is not sufficient by itself. Freeze the reviewer authority, available tools, prohibited sources for downstream roles, prior-exposure handling, review output destination and resumption behavior before substantive independent adjudication. If no suitable reviewer is available, preserve HOLD rather than relabel task-owner analysis as independent evidence.

## 3. Source record and adjudication contract candidate

Review **all candidate primary author inputs**, not merely the 2,133 new records. The current 406-carrier candidate also lacks universal proof of authority, currentness and absence of every semantic leakage route. The 2,133-record inventory supplies the presently missing historical-family obligations.

Every prospective verdict must bind the exact historical source revision and enough evidence to reproduce the decision:

- historical commit; canonical source path; full Git blob OID, size and digest; supported representation and any derived excerpt's original line spans;
- source family and actual content role, including Product versus Project, natural owner, proposal/decision/specification/implementation/test/receipt/derived-view distinctions;
- scope, lifecycle, historical-versus-current claim, supersession/effective date where supported and independent evidence for those interpretations;
- contamination class, precise bounded source span/evidence, potential exposure of old W5 relevance lists, Research 217 answer membership, R0-P03 tasks, rubric, arm definition or expected comparator result;
- permitted author-facing representation (full exact blob, reviewed exact original excerpt, auxiliary-only, prohibited, unsupported, or held);
- reviewer provenance, uncertainty, any necessary second review, evidence limits and a nonanswer-leaking public receipt.

Separate **what a source actually says** from **what authority the source has**. The location `docs/DECISIONS.md` does not make every clause active; source-code existence does not prove deployed J2 realization; a test's text does not prove its PASS; a J3-generated projection does not certify freshness. An old accepted record may be evidence of historical authority without being the current governing rule.

Do not put old gold, expected-answer prose, raw spoiler excerpts or reviewer-private output into the public decision manifest. If an exclusion explanation would reveal answers, preserve a sanitized public reason and retain any sensitive rationale under separately approved reviewer-only custody, not the private companion by default.

## 4. Proposed dispositions

These are review states, **not automatic author admissions**:

| Review disposition | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `CANDIDATE_FULL` | The complete original blob may enter the proposed neutral author world only after an actual evidence-cited reviewer decision and final cohort validation. |
| `CANDIDATE_EXCERPT` | A narrowly defined exact source span may be proposed when source facts and contamination are inseparable in a larger document; origin path@blob and original line numbers must remain verifiable. No fabricated paraphrase may masquerade as raw source. |
| `AUXILIARY_EVIDENCE_ONLY` | May support non-arm adjudication or audit, but is not included in the independent author's input. It cannot secretly repair an author-visible answer gap. |
| `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD` | Explicit original relevance/gold or R0-P03 designer/scorer leakage cannot enter the primary task author's world. The reason is tied to actual reviewed content, not only path regex. |
| `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW` | Source authority, status, contamination or required completeness cannot be established sufficiently. |
| `UNSUPPORTED_REPRESENTATION` | Source has not been faithfully inspected in the required modality. This is not a finding of irrelevance. |

A flagged file may ultimately contain a safe bounded original excerpt or necessary historical fact; an unflagged file may still be contaminated. Either result needs content-grounded justification. Mixed contaminated documents must not be silently summarized by an exposed coordinator into a favorable primary-task answer. A proposed excerpt requires a deterministically auditable, no-hidden-context transformation; otherwise hold it.

No decision may change Research 513 thresholds, inject B1/B2/B3/B4 advantages into the shared baseline, move Research 217's historic 65-carrier world into the primary cohort, or select sources because they answer designer-preferred scenarios. The final 60/20 procedure and 12 task origination remain future prospective freezes.

## 5. Review ordering, scale and falsification

1. Freeze the non-arm reviewer instruction and classification schema **before** handing it scored-author source content. Show exact source pin, manifest digests, all six excluded evidence families, scope of the 406 existing sources and prohibited old-gold/design roots.
2. Verify a small non-scored calibration spanning real statuses and synthetic hazard types, including: an apparently harmless historical proposal, current-vs-former governing conflict, implementation-versus-validation, stale J3 view, an old W5 answer-bearing source, a newer R0-P03 design-bearing source, a mixed useful/contaminated record, a no-lexical-flag semantic spoiler and an unsupported modality. Do not reuse the four author rehearsal questions as scored seeds.
3. Use deterministic family/role batching for review logistics, not auto-admission. Expose full source text only to the status reviewer within its declared review surface, keeping reviewer-private spoiler evidence out of downstream task-author materials. Preserve exact decisions and counterexamples for disputed cases.
4. For every proposed author-visible source or excerpt, independently check original Git identity and adjudication completeness. A record with missing provenance, unresolved contamination, unsupported content or ambiguous currentness **cannot** silently enter the source world.
5. Qualify cross-plane Product/Project, accepted/proposed, J1/J2/J3, AO/WARRANT-F, evidence/validation, evolution and recovery coverage as **source-role and answerability capacity diagnostics**, not task prompts or source selection optimized after knowing future task answers.
6. Subject source-status conflicts or nontrivial mixed-content decisions to a separate qualified challenge/second disposition before a final admission. No arbitrary percentage of unreviewed text becomes automatically eligible merely because scanning throughput is limited.
7. If exclusion hides evidence necessary for truthful source closure or source coverage is unknown, report `HOLD_SOURCE_WORLD_INCOMPLETE`; do not substitute an easier task or claim full coverage. If 80 representative carriers cannot be justified, retain Research 513's prospective insufficiency/fallback obligations.
8. Only after review and an exact candidate manifest have been independently accepted may the author-facing source world, metadata, restrictions and fresh-author task-neutral brief be frozen. Task selection and reference adjudication follow separately; the original legacy 65-carrier world remains distinct.

Validation must attack both false admissions and false exclusions. Negative lexical screens are not proof of safety, and catalog inclusion is not proof of governing authority. Every reviewer decision is provisional until the final independent admission and resulting exact closed-world representation are checked.
