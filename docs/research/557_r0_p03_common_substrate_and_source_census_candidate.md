## 1. Why this candidate exists

Research 513 Section 6 requires all navigation arms to operate over an identical V03-native substrate, lexical full-text and connector-readable derived orientation. Research 556 identified two first-order risks: the physical successor V03 is not yet deployed, and an apparently neutral experiment might (a) privilege a Research 217 subject taxonomy or (b) weaken B0 by withholding selected V03 relations so B2 appears to improve performance merely by restoring them.

The current stage therefore resolves **the test-representation and sampling feasibility design**, not the performance of a navigation arm. Code here may be used to audit candidate public source pools; it may **not** promote current Markdown prose to J1 accepted-effect authority.

The system goal remains the best integrated professional ADS architecture across semantic organization, AO, WARRANT-F, development tooling, information ownership, migration, delivery and recovery. This is not a simplicity-first rule or an experiment about finding a nice folder taxonomy.

## 5. Common comparison substrate: what every arm receives

The shared JSON candidate defines these **common logical records**, each tied to exact public source/fixture evidence:

1. **Carrier**: source path, Git commit/blob evidence, information role, natural owner, explicit evidence class and currentness/authority source basis. Optional canonical identity and temporal/supersession refs are present only when independently justified. A path or source label cannot mint semantic identity or accepted authority.
2. **Governing effect**: exact governed subject/effect reference, scope, status, lineage and source provenance. Where a future V03 concept is not actually instantiated today, its test analogue must be expressly **synthetic** and treated equally across arms. An exact governed subject is distinct from a B1 topical `subject` or B3 cross-cutting concern.
3. **Shared relation**: V03 accepted semantics, lineage/currentness, completion, J2 realization/coverage, evidence/receipts and AO-relevant dependency links, with edge provenance. The candidate's relation-family labels are illustrative, **not** new accepted production enums. Derived reverse and closure edges carry their originating provenance and no additional authority.
4. **Connector-readable derived orientation**: common bounded current-authority summary, provenance/source checkpoint, staleness visibility and navigation entry points, explicitly labeled **non-authoritative**. All arms receive the same interface and view content before optional augmentations.

The **source of truth is not manufactured by the experiment**. A source-derived currentness conclusion requires actual evidence; anything unknown must remain explicitly unresolved. The common fixture is an *evaluation model of selected semantic contracts*, not a hidden authoritative successor deployment.

Every arm receives identical source data/roles, base lexical retrieval and structural graph access, authority labels, source revisions, freshness information, derived orientation and tool/context budgets. No arm's additional membership, link, concern or similarity signal becomes J1 authority.

## 6. What counts as an actual experimental difference?

- **B0** gets the complete baseline above; it may not be reduced to an artificially weak keyword-only search. Its selected V03 typed relations, deterministic orientation and source identity must remain usable.
- **B1** adds Research 217 controlled topical vocabulary, source-owned membership, polyhierarchy and a preferred navigation route. The historical annotation work is included in total authoring-cost evidence; historical 9/9 is separate legacy evidence, not a P03 result.
- **B2** adds **editorial source-owned carrier-to-governing `about` navigation links only when they provide a meaningful source-discovery relationship absent from common V03 governing, J2 coverage, lineage and evidence edges**. The links may help navigate narrative evidence to governed effects but never assert realization, acceptance or policy authority. If all feasible B2 links restate B0, the arm is non-distinct and should be prospectively collapsed or its comparator design revisited **before scored results**. Do not fabricate redundant links just to preserve the arm count.
- **B3** adds a small explicit cross-cutting concern vocabulary with optional assignments. A concern is *not* an exact J1 subject, file path, lifecycle status, information role or broad membership inherited automatically from B1. The actual distinction and editorial cost remain to be demonstrated.
- **B4** adds provenance-labeled semantic retrieval to one prospectively chosen justified structured arm. It cannot elevate probabilistic relevance to authority, receive privileged hidden context, or have implementation feasibility decided after viewing scores. Research 513 permits omission **before** scoring if equalized realization is not achievable.

This preserves the independent-design insights of Research 508 and Claude Message 001 without an automatic duplicate ChatGPT-versus-Claude architecture contest.

## 8. Findings, alternative interpretations and review triggers

**The most important unresolved issues are:**

1. Is the proposed ten-stratum public population appropriate, or does it undersample a crucial semantic/currentness class while overrepresenting checkpoint/research history? The 60/20 quota is from Research 513; the ten equal 6/2 strata are *ours*, explicitly challengeable.
2. Is there enough real source evidence to construct a **neutral V03 analogue** without inventing production J1 effects or predetermining relation-first navigation? If not, amend the representation/corpus before experiments, not after observing scores.
3. Is B2 a real additional mechanism over the chosen V03 substrate? If it collapses, prospective redesign, merge or explicit alternative-family assessment is legitimate; do not keep a ceremonial null arm.
4. Does B3 express a genuinely distinct, independently maintainable navigation abstraction relative to Research 217's broader topical membership?
5. How can prospective cohort/sample/task custody be kept independent under available tools? The current ChatGPT session has seen the Research 217 historical answers and designed this source-frame method. Claude's existing comparative thread has substantial R0 design exposure. Neither qualifies automatically as an unexposed scorer.
6. Does the proposed 80-path source frame have enough current-authority evidence vs prior proposals? What relevant private facts must be tested only through approved public projections?
7. What fairness method permits B4 with matched implementation and authoring investment without exposing model pretraining/prior tool advantages as scored navigation performance?

These are material **design-critique questions**, not an automatic gate to another lengthy multi-model document-audit campaign. The benefit of independent Claude critique would be to challenge the shared substrate, the ten-stratum frame and the B0/B2/B3 meaning; this is appropriate if an actual independent critique channel is available. No Claude response has been requested or received in this checkpoint.
