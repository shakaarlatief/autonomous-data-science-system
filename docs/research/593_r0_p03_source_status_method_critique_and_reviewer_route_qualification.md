## 2. Falsification of the Research 592 method

Research 592 correctly rejects blanket admission, lexical auto-admission and pretending that the exposed coordinator can provide fresh independent judgments. However, its provisional dispositions and exact-span requirement leave several material false-PASS routes unresolved:

| Failure mode | Falsifying counterexample | Required prospective repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temporal collapse | A historical document accurately calls a predecessor `ACTIVE`, while a later pinned decision distinguishes accepted logical target from deployed operational contract | Bind source event/claim time, historical-pin observation time and requested evaluation-as-of time separately; never import today's live state into a historical assertion |
| Qualifier-loss in exact excerpt | A quoted sentence says a mechanism was accepted, but its following paragraph limits acceptance to a non-operational candidate | Exact line ranges alone do not prove semantic completeness; reviewer must identify governing qualifiers, negations, exceptions, conflict/succession edges and necessary surrounding source context, or HOLD |
| Metadata leakage | A publicly published source name, path, exclusion description or membership/disposition mapping discloses a legacy answer theme or R0-P03 comparator | Treat paths, role tags, line offsets, roster order, batch membership and rejection reasons as potential spoiler metadata; publish only an independently checked nonanswer-bearing public summary |
| Screening-to-safety promotion | One of 2,008 files lacks tested lexical flags but contains semantic task-design guidance without the tested vocabulary | A negative lexical screen is workload routing only; no `CANDIDATE_FULL` or excerpt eligibility without actual semantic review and provenance |

## 3. Temporal and source-status decision contract

Each source-status assessment must distinguish at least:

1. **Historical content pin:** immutable `c57556a8bf993a1d327e9197acb7b7c745071cd8`, canonical path, exact Git blob OID, supported byte representation, SHA-256 and original lines where applicable.
2. **Source assertion time:** the time or interval the passage itself claims, including explicit `UNKNOWN` when none can be supported. File creation date, Git commit time and content-effective time are not interchangeable.
3. **Assessment as-of:** historical pin or another explicitly authorized request time. Current live Checkpoint 898 cannot be silently projected back into a historical answer; conversely, an old state label cannot supersede current accepted routing.
4. **Role and authority:** Product/Project, natural owner, declaration/proposal/owner decision/governing contract/implementation/test/observed validation/derived view, scope, authenticity limits, accepted-versus-operational distinction, and exact cited evidence or `UNRESOLVED`.
5. **Dependency context:** pertinent qualifiers, negation, supersession, counterevidence, known disputed status and missing J2/J3 validation. An exact excerpt proposal requires evidence that omitted context does not change its meaning.

The reviewer must not turn a requirement to explain historical architecture into a requirement to invent today's governing truth. Authority assertions remain independently adjudicable and may be unknown even where the underlying source quote is genuine.

## 4. Dispositions, separate from the final author-facing cohort

Retain Research 592's six candidate dispositions: `CANDIDATE_FULL`, `CANDIDATE_EXCERPT`, `AUXILIARY_EVIDENCE_ONLY`, `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD`, `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW` and `UNSUPPORTED_REPRESENTATION`. Add an independent **review-depth status**, separate **uncertainty**, **challenge status** and **release gate**. A disposition marked candidate is never itself an admission.

To propose a full source or excerpt, the reviewer must attest actual inspection of relevant full source content and provenance, not a path-based role hint. A full-source proposal also requires no unresolved material contamination or authority ambiguity that changes the author's legitimate interpretation. A mixed source can receive exact excerpt treatment only if all controlling qualifiers are preserved and its location metadata, introduction and ordering do not leak forbidden outcomes. Otherwise HOLD or auxiliary-only; do not substitute a rewritten answer.

The source-status process should have an **author-input construction phase separate from reviewer disposition**. That gate mechanically verifies immutable roster, exact bytes, source/batch provenance, excerpt reproducibility, absence of prohibited reviewer-private content in the author packet, and representation parity. It cannot independently certify semantic neutrality merely from hashes or text pattern scans. A challenged admission, unknown context closure or uncontrolled side channel prevents a claimed final freeze.

## 5. Proportional coverage without silent admissions

The mandatory standard is **complete accounted-for population**, not necessarily identical full-text model attention to each of 2,539 records. Deterministic metadata-based triage may prioritize known contamination, governing/decision and cross-plane source roles, disputed statuses, missing evidence and supported modalities. Reviewer batches must record precise coverage, unfinished items, resource limits and whether the reviewer actually had the entire file. There is **no new numeric review quota, random seed, trial score, task count substitution or revised scientific gate** in this Research. Any future sampling/coverage threshold must be prospectively challenged and explicitly justified rather than optimized against obtained performance.

## 6. Dual-boundary reviewer records and publication

The reviewer's **restricted evidence ledger**, once an actual output location/role is qualified, may hold source path@OID, exact reviewed content coordinates, nuanced lifecycle evidence, contamination spans, disagreements and challenged verdicts. It may contain old answer/designer material; therefore it cannot be committed to public ADS, silently stored in the project private companion, or passed to the future fresh task author. A user-facing transcript or log with that material is not an automatic approved custodian.

The **public nonanswer-bearing progress receipt** may record source pin, review method version, aggregate count by family/review depth/hold/disposition, unsupported/coverage limits, independent reviewer role/status and public commitment to the restricted record. It must not include raw spoiler excerpts, source-to-gold associations, case-by-case old relevance sets, reviewer-specific answer hints or an unreviewed-path whitelist that functions as a gold map. Publishing a digest without independently verifiable complete restricted bytes is not evidence of recoverable custody.

The eventual **author input world** is a third separately constructed, frozen, exact-source packet with its own permitted metadata and reviewer-approved full files/excerpts. It must not inherit the restricted ledger's annotations, hazard labels, answer cues or excluded-source inventory by default. Even otherwise accurate source-path and excerpt-line metadata must be scrutinized for answer leakage.
