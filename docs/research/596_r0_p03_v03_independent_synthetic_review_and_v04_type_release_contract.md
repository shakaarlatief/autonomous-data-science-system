## 3. Cross-cutting false admissions, false exclusions and author surface

The critic's original fictional adversarial cases expose additional substantive insufficiency:

- A Markdown hash may remain unchanged while a linked or viewer-loaded asset changes to contain a protected outcome. Dynamic assets, redirects, hover/search/citation/download metadata and actual rendered author interface must be qualified as *exposure surface*, not inferred from one Git blob.
- An inert or provably immaterial citation must not create an infinite inspection requirement. Faithfully bounded author-view behavior distinguishes loaded, navigable, material-context and inert references.
- Two sources can form a context-complete **joint bundle** even if neither is independently sufficient. Conditional bundling avoids a preauthorization deadlock without approving a bundle prematurely.
- A historical decision or observed site cutover does not establish global deployment at every environment, version, time or population. Precise applicability domains and non-inferred activation remain mandatory.
- A hidden, unreviewed contemporary exception can invalidate a false “context complete” source claim. A bounded coverage and negative-search witness is needed for the declared source universe; absence of found conflicts is not proof of absent exceptions.
- A disclosure spoiler can be irrelevant to the focal factual claim but remain material to the author's exposure. Claim materiality and disclosure materiality must be independent.
- Public progress counts can disclose one private classification through differencing, even when each receipt appears innocuous. Publication-history and external-join analysis cannot be a one-file regex check.
- A historical proposal remains evidence that the proposal existed, even if later deployment failed; safe narrow claims must not be needlessly suppressed by unrelated uncertainty.
