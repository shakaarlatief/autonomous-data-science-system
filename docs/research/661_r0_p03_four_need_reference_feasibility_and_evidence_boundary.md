## 4. Interpretation and decision-relevant implication

This was one deliberate fixed, nonrepresentative **four-category feasibility pilot**, not a probability sample of real user demand and not an independently proven answerability rate. Its denominator is precisely four committed questions; `1/4` must never be treated as an R0-P03 arm score, a representative success rate, a calibrated model-accuracy estimate or a threshold prediction for the formal twelve-case protocol.

The pilot shows that independent need origination from neutral filenames is operationally possible, and that a separate historical-source reviewer can produce some citation-bearing evidence. It also demonstrates the importance of scoping the **information demand**, preserving authority/temporal uncertainty, and budgeting complete *primary evidence chains* rather than merely finding relevant-looking documents. A single wide request to enumerate every cross-plane route, all indirect dependencies or all operative provisions is substantially different from a focused decision, even when its question is realistic and useful. The historical reference result should not be retrospectively made greener by narrowing those questions.

We should **not** repeat another broad fresh-question-origination loop or reopen the same historical searches without a new, prospectively justified information gain. The sensible progression is bounded **independent source-citation/claim fidelity qualification** under a separate controlled local-only access plan, followed by a concrete, *unscored* source-to-answer/navigation feasibility test against this frozen evidence where a fair leakage boundary can actually be maintained. If the private source-line audit cannot be performed without exposing case-specific truth to a future scored-arm author, report a protected-evidence HOLD instead of claiming scored gold. Any future twelve-primary/legacy nine-case design must be independently prospectively qualified; a redesign is not a retroactive passing result.

The accepted architectural freedom at every level remains unchanged: repository layout, documentation, semantic mechanism, code, source ownership, CI/CD, branching, collaboration and physical target are all revisable under explicit evidence and governance. No physical target, production authority or migration cutover is selected or authorized by this pilot.
