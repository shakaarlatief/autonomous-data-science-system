## 5. Prospective experiment construction: obligations before exact freeze

1. **Separate common substrate truth from comparator augmentations.** Freeze a canonical test fact model grounded in accepted V03 identities/relations plus represented current repository source/revision status. Verify B0 already supports baseline graph/lexical capability, typed owners, provenance, lifecycle, AO/WARRANT-F reachability and connector-readable orientation. Distinguish `DECLARED_SOURCE`, `ACCEPTED_CURRENT`, `HISTORICAL`, `DERIVED` and `SYNTHETIC_PROBE` evidence as appropriate; this list is a candidate representation vocabulary, not an approved new status enum.
2. **Source eligibility and sampling.** Select a representative, role-stratified sample of ~80 current tracked carriers with purposefully covered governing meaning, natural-owner evidence, operational procedures, architecture-evolution history, AO, WARRANT-F, Product/Project, migration and recovery. Freeze an objective or reviewable sampling rule and a deterministic 60/20 build/held-out split *before arm construction*. If eligible universe is smaller, use Research 513's all-qualifying-carriers 3:1 rule. Do not count 854 research paths as 854 eligible target J1 records; no sample selection by favoring old B1 paths.
3. **Twelve new, independent primary tasks.** Freeze three each of (a) cold start/current orientation, (b) cross-plane discovery and AO-relevant context, (c) impact analysis/architecture evolution/migration, and (d) exact source, authority, historical recovery. Task prompts and provenance/answer oracles must derive from an independent pre-arm source/history analysis or a mechanically frozen historical owner-question/event selection rule. The scenario author must not design the compared arms. Avoid mere old category-label lookups; use multi-hop whole-system questions.
4. **Legacy regression separately.** Keep Research 217's nine scenarios and exact historical fixtures byte-addressable. Resolve present-scope applicability and time/revision interpretation prospectively without replacing the original 9/9 versus 4/9 evidence. No primary-task leakage from existing `must_retrieve` examples.
5. **Authoring and maintenance fairness.** Give B1/B2/B3 actual recorded source-authoring effort and existing sunk metadata costs, equal build time/effort constraints as required by the comparator protocol, prospective metadata rules and explicit held-out update mechanics. Measure generated vs authored ratio, whether adding held-out carriers forces edits to prior metadata, and deletion/rebuild of derived indexes. No generated ranking or index receives authority.
6. **Fresh evaluators and blindness.** For judgement-requiring tasks, at least two evaluators who authored no arm; keep other-arm answers, answer keys and source-preparation privileged notes hidden until their own evaluation is frozen. Same task order policy, source-access permissions, tool/read and context budgets, orientation publication and provenance format. Separate mechanical correctness checks where possible. Current ChatGPT has seen Research 217's historical fixture, Research 513 and arm descriptions; Claude/claude-04 has participated in past comparative design. Neither is automatically a *fresh blind evaluator* simply because they are different models.
7. **Exact-scoring oracle.** Before scored results, define exact-source/revision/correct-authority conditions, partial/false-authority cases, relevant-source recall/irrelevant-context, tool and token counters, human browseability rubric, generated-view deletion, and AO-retrieval boundary. Authored oracle independent from candidate implementation, validated via positive and adversarial negative source examples.
8. **Immutable source/version boundaries and safety.** Pin public source files/commit or blob digests and scenario/corpus/arm/answer-key boundaries separately. Never read or expose private companion data to evaluators without explicit scope authority; use permitted public projections. No production J1 mutation, source migration or proposed answer may silently become accepted governing fact by participating in this experiment.

These are concrete design obligations, **not** eight automatic new review campaigns. Implementation choice and validation depth should follow actual discriminating risk. An external vector/embedding service is neither mandated nor forbidden; its feasibility and fairness must be decided prospectively.

## 6. Preserve Research 513 scoring and decision thresholds

Unchanged: **12 new primary tasks** (3 per category), **9 historical tasks**, **at least two fresh evaluators** for human/model judgments, controlled equalized arm treatment, source citations, zero false-authority/safety failures. Else prefer the simpler viable arm, or preserve multiple non-dominated viable arms for owner review under the preregistered PASS/PASS_WITH_SELECTION/AMEND/REOPEN/INVALID classes.
