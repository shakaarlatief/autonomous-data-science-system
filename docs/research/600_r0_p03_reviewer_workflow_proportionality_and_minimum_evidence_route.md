## 1. Governing question and the proportionality correction

R0-P03 is an **architecture-comparison experiment**. The S1 review exists to establish a defensible, contamination-safe original-source world for a genuinely fresh primary task author, then independent reference adjudication and prospective B0–B4 scoring. It is not, by itself, a project to build a general-purpose confidential multi-agent operating system.

Research 513/559 demand independently grounded tasks and references, arm-neutral evidence and unchanged hard viability rules. Research 590/591 identify **406 candidate sources** and **2,133 additional historical records** (2,539 accounted-for across those two enumerated populations, with other unsupported modalities tracked separately); none is author admitted. Research 592/593 define a separate source-status reviewer capable of seeing potentially contaminated material and **therefore excluded from later supposedly blind task-author/reference/evaluator roles**. The reviewer must not be substituted by the exposed engineering coordinator.

Research 575/576 expressly favor **Level B controlled independence** over open-ended whole-agent sandboxing for *arm author execution only*. That owner decision does **not** automatically waive the source-status reviewer's separate role/custody conditions. It does, however, establish a project-level principle of proportionality: do not demand increasingly strong infrastructure when an actual risk and an actual required evidence property can be satisfied by a simpler measured workflow. We must never invent a global requirement for two Windows accounts when no governing decision requires that particular mechanism.

**Coordinator assessment:** Research 597–599's synthetic policy/OS reconnaissance was legitimate and discovered real design limitations. However, making the proposed two-principal Windows trial the **default next step** would mistake one possible enforcement implementation for the scientific requirement. The smallest next useful work is to qualify an *actual reviewer-to-owner-to-author information boundary* with precisely scoped, independently recoverable evidence and transparent residual risk. Windows ACLs and service authentication remain escalation options when a demonstrated material failure makes them beneficial.

## 3. Minimum S1 evidence obligations, independent of technology

**A. Actor and exposure boundary.** Identify a genuinely separate non-arm source-status reviewer and qualified challenger, record which tools, chat/project memory, source repositories, previous material and connected apps they could access, and retain their disqualification from downstream blinded roles. New sessions offer practical hygiene, not proof of no latent training/provider exposure. The owner remains custodian and approval authority. Do not claim that a Windows token can attest a model's internal history.

**B. Pinned input and genuine inspection.** Review against exact historical source pin `c57556a8bf993a1d327e9197acb7b7c745071cd8`, canonical path@blob, source/representation hashes, declared complete batch manifest and a missing/truncation report. Review *every accounted-for population item* at a documented inspection depth or HOLD, not necessarily all full-text in one context window. Mechanical lexical filters route work only, never admit sources.

**C. Reviewer-private recoverable output.** Preserve the full source-specific assessment, including potentially answer-bearing reasons, in a deliberately selected *owner-controlled or equivalently restricted* destination inaccessible to any later blind author and not handed to the exposed coordinator. Verify exact bytes and readback across the intended recovery process. Protect version history and independent commitment against rollback commensurate with the chosen threat model. Public Git and ordinary coordinator chat cannot become default private ledgers.

**D. Separate challenge and author release.** A different qualified reviewer challenges material conflicts, contested excerpts, missing governing context, temporal currentness and disclosure leaks. Author-candidate statuses are not author release. The final frozen, original-source author packet, exact render, metadata and cumulative publication history require an independent release check. No protected reviewer explanation repairs an author-incomplete source world.

**E. Feasible, proportionate implementation.** Use actual model-facing tool and storage routes, with positive recovery and negative source-leak checks that distinguish provider/app restrictions, Codexless registry refusal, Windows ACL denial and missing files. Do not demand a particular OS, account count, key service, branch model, private repo, authentication vendor or tooling style without evidence that it is needed.

These are **required properties to demonstrate**, not requirements to implement one maximal system. Unknown actor accessibility or unqualified confidential output remains HOLD for real contaminated-source delivery, but not for ordinary source-free engineering.

| **M2: separate Windows principal/ACL-backed local ledger** | More concrete OS denial and local custody tests; can be layered behind M1 if actual reviewer pathway uses it | Does not itself authenticate a remote AI role or prevent model-side exposure, adds lifecycle/cleanup complexity | **Defer** until a specific protected-access limitation or chosen actual route warrants it |

**Assessment:** M1 is the highest-value next *qualification candidate*, because it tests the **actual information movement into and out of a reviewer session** rather than a local OS denial detached from the eventual AI workflow. This is not an assertion that Temporary Chat intrinsically provides protected storage. Any real confidential content requires the later approved owner-only/equivalent private output path, and actual provider/history/tool exposure limitations must be openly bounded.

## 5. One specific missing-property trial, not more general rehearsals

Research 589 covered transfer of a **fixed fake reference packet**; Research 594 covered a **fictional source-review report pasted openly**. Their combined evidence does **not** prove that a reviewer can preserve a **structured, complete, potentially sensitive reviewer-status ledger** through a protected handoff while the exposed coordinator sees **only** a safe nonanswer-bearing receipt.

A single future fictional, **reviewer-specific** end-to-end handoff is therefore justified **if** its plan explicitly tests this *new* property:

1. Owner authorizes exact temporary tools, publicly distributable key/certificate if encryption is chosen, output format and destination, without revealing the owner's private key. Existing fake-only keys and sender templates may serve only as technical reference, not be silently reused for protected use.
2. A separately opened session reports its actual access/exposure, reads a synthetic immutable batch with mixed fictional source statuses, emits a full **fictional restricted** report with at least one fake protected cue and a safe public receipt, and applies the prospective typed V0.4 method only as a calibration candidate.
3. The full response is packaged for **owner-only recovery**, with exact source manifest/version, output bytes/digest, reviewer session/limitations, pending/disputed statuses and interruption/accounting record. The exposed coordinator receives *no raw restricted report*. A separate authorized challenger route is rehearsed if it can preserve the same boundary without multiplying exposure.
4. Owner independently reads back the recovered complete report and checks exact count/hash/version, and checks which fields may safely be public. A direct actual author-route test must later verify absence of the reviewer report and excluded source metadata, rather than inferring secrecy from nonsharing.
5. Each failed operation or unknown exposure produces a scoped HOLD. A successful fictional trial qualifies only that transfer/output workflow, not real-source admission, general confidentiality or authority to score.

Do **not** create or dispatch this trial by merely publishing a template. In particular, the owner must approve the intended output-custody destination, sender/recipient mechanism and actual reviewer tool/session route **before** sending any genuinely contaminating historical file.

The separately reviewed V0.4 method is still pending. One focused synthetic challenge may be useful, but only if it changes a material acceptance decision; repeat cycles without new falsification value should stop.

## 6. Explicit architectural trigger discipline and stopping rules

Upgrade from M1 toward M2/M3/M4 when directly observed evidence shows any of:

- Reviewer response cannot be preserved completely and recovered independently without protected plaintext appearing in exposed coordinator views or app logs.
- Reviewer session/tool cannot be bounded to the actual pinned input or a substantial known contaminating external surface remains accessible.
- An actual later blind author can access the restricted reviewer ledger, an old gold source, or a direct mapping hint through its permitted tools/context.
- Owner-controlled custody loses or silently rewrites challenge decisions across restarts; independent commitments cannot detect rollback.
- Reviewer/challenger identities cannot be separated credibly, essential batch sizes exceed usable semantic inspection capacity, or source rendering changes after manifest checks.
- Public receipt publication or exact author-view metadata makes a protected inference, even if the source body is omitted.
- Another architecture measurably meets the same obligations with lower risk, cost and owner involvement.

Conversely, **stop adding security infrastructure** if a concrete end-to-end reviewer workflow demonstrates sufficient bounded role/exposure, exact source transport, durable private report recovery, second challenge and author-source exclusion for the specific declared threat model. No additional Windows/VM/service isolation follows automatically from a PASS.

The architecture and engineering/work procedures remain **redesignable at every level**, including repo/layout, branches, CI/CD, tool routes and participant handoffs. The immutable part is the scientific validity and evidence needed to claim it, not the current method of implementing the boundary.
