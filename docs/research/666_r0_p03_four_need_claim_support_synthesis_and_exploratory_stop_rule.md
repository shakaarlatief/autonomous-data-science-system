## 5. Explicit stop-rule for the current four-need exploratory loop

The four-question pipeline has now produced real original-source search results, eight original answer texts, source-line and case-boundary checks, a masked claim-support reviewer report, and a complete source-free synthesis. It has exposed useful problems:

1. **Answer-relevant source closure is insufficient.** Neither ranker/answerer covered an entire complex need under fixed excerpt budgets, particularly historical state/decision authority, changes and source-to-implementation witnesses.
2. **Citation support is not complete authority reconstruction.** A genuine cited line may be an earlier recorded assertion, an unverified promotion candidate or a narrow design claim.
3. **Case-local source boundaries matter.** The B N03 cross-question use affected nine claim units, and the authors' original interfaces allowed them to see unrelated case sources.
4. **Claim-count statistics can mislead.** The 170 supported labels include limitations and proper abstentions, and the reviewer is from the same model family.
5. **A heading diagnostic is not the proposed knowledge architecture.** It cannot establish whether source-owned facets, editorial links, selective concerns or semantic retrieval justify their life-cycle costs.

**Coordinator recommendation and next engineering priority:** **STOP commissioning additional near-identical four-need source-answer reruns and same-family reviews for this cohort.** Further runs would have little independent architecture-decision value unless a new mechanism or falsifiable admission blocker is identified. This stop applies only to the **exploratory four-question route**, not R0-P03 or the project's ability to redesign itself.

Next continue **substantive source-eligibility/role/lifecycle/authority qualification and common V03-native substrate parity engineering** as already authorized for non-scored preparation by Research 576, and specified in Research 559. The next bounded technical artifact should inventory which of the four original task families can obtain **actual source/authority/implementation closure** from representative original J1/J2/J3 source roles and whether a common non-arm extractor can provide an identical source-identity, currentness, lineage, effect/owner, receipt/authority and connector-readable orientation substrate for every future official arm. The implementation may be redesigned at every level, including files, storage, schemas, tests, CI/CD, Git workflow and multi-model operations.

This is **not** permission to bypass formal role admission, privately authored source-neutral gold, genuinely fresh independent evaluators, sealed maintenance samples, or equal real B0–B4 arm implementation. Research 576 explicitly allows only non-scored Level C engineering when Level B independence requirements are unmet.
