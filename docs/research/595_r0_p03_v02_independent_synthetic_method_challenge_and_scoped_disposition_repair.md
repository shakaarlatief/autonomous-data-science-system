## 3. Proposed V0.3 decision semantics

The new contract is `experiments/r0_p03_navigation_probe/NON_ARM_SOURCE_STATUS_REVIEWER_CONTRACT_CANDIDATE_V03.md`. It preserves the original six disposition names and S0–S4 gates, but makes five decision dimensions independent: exact delivered representation and semantic inspection depth; evidenced source assertion/role/time/authority; contamination and blocked uncertainty; proposed disclosure scope (file/span/representation/metadata/packet); and candidate-versus-independent-challenge-versus-release authorization.

The disposition is now a **scope-specific proposal**, not an inherent attribute of a path. One file can be prohibited in full, held for one excerpt, proposed for another excerpt, useful in reviewer-only audit and unsafe in published metadata without contradiction. The record must preserve those distinct scopes. The new rules prevent an author-visible label from promoting a proposal to operational truth or a gold reference.

For each affected scope: missing bytes imply `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_REVIEW`; delivered but uninterpretable representation implies `UNSUPPORTED_REPRESENTATION` at that representation, with downstream dependent scopes held; confirmed prohibited material in the proposed exposure implies `PROHIBIT_DESIGN_OR_GOLD`; unresolved materially governing context or potential contamination implies HOLD; an authorized reviewer-only treatment requires a **specific separate disclosure exclusion reason** for `AUXILIARY_EVIDENCE_ONLY`; and only adequately inspected, context-complete, nonleaking original content may be proposed as `CANDIDATE_FULL` or `CANDIDATE_EXCERPT`. None is final author admission.

The contract distinguishes material and nonmaterial uncertainties **per claim and proposed use**. An unknown deployment date blocks a deployment-currentness assertion; it need not erase a narrower verifiable historical fact that a selection was proposed. This preserves genuine source capacity without covertly relaxing independence or answerability rules.

## 4. Dependency closure, temporal authority and disclosure invalidation

A span candidate requires exact Git-original text; controlling qualifiers, supersession, negation, metadata and context may live outside the chosen lines or source. If the reviewer has seen a controlling exception only in restricted evidence, the exact excerpt is **not author-context-complete**. No reviewer-only paraphrase can repair a missing author-visible source. A permit/deny decision must record dependencies and whether every material needed qualifier can be supplied safely.

Effective-date reconstruction must keep publication, claim, decision, proposed effective, independently evidenced activation/realization, supersession and requested as-of distinct. Unknown or conflicting authority is recorded as unresolved, not resolved by recency, file location, old checkpoint label or assumed no rollback.

Candidate author-packet construction is its own release scope. It is checked after component candidates: cross-file conclusions, missing exception, source ordering, joinable IDs, filenames, redacted/excluded paths, line labels and differencing with earlier receipts may create leakage or false authority. A changed source disposition or context witness invalidates its dependent candidate span/packet until reviewed again. Public output can be only a separately challenged, nonanswer-bearing receipt; all detailed hazards remain reviewer-only under **future separately approved custody**.
