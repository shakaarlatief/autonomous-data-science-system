This would need a new exact owner-visible decision and independent semantic justification that it does not silently waive Research 512, 513, 576 or 673 authority controls. 3. **Retain HOLD and select a different real source scenario or explicitly amend the experimental target.** The existing result remains a valid negative/unknown source case, but it cannot be silently promoted to a complete qualified S1 positive source world. Any formal-method change requires a separately bounded owner decision.

Until that exact decision and additional proof, S1 stays dormant and J3 cannot certify coverage. The independent source corpus/arms and protected primary/legacy reference conditions remain to be qualified. `GOVERNED_LEDGER_KERNEL_V02` remains only a leading physical candidate.
