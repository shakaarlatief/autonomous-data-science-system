## 2. Architectural position

THIN_CENTRED_HYBRID_V01 is:

    a thin governing-semantics layer
    + exact references to bounded domain-native contracts
    + closed acceptance-time realization accounting
    + natural-owner realization declarations
    + deterministic operational predicates
    + governed N:M requirement lineage
    + compact generated orientation
    + independent detective controls

It is not:

    a universal Project schema
    a universal ObligationUnit database
    a prose-to-authority inference engine
    a birth-time implementation grouping system
    an authored global realization-state database
    a replacement for AO, WARRANT-F, Git, continuity/recovery or other natural domain contracts.

The architecture remains a component of the Project System.

It is not the ADS Product's data-science reasoning architecture.

## 4. One accepted meaning boundary

A governing acceptance is one semantic acceptance boundary.

It may physically bind several components:

    human-readable carrier revision
    accepted operative consequence component
    exact accepted domain-contract revisions

Those components are accepted together as one governing meaning boundary.

Generated JSON, indexes, projections, databases, ActionContracts and orientation views have no independent semantic authority.

If accepted components conflict:

    acceptance validation fails
    OR
    a post-acceptance defect/evolution route opens.

A later model does not choose precedence by confidence or prose interpretation.

## 5. J1 accepted-effect identity and granularity

Every accepted machine-trackable governing effect receives a stable:

    accepted_effect_id

For REQUIRE semantics:

    one independently acceptable governing effect
        -> one stable REQUIRE identity.

An independently acceptable effect is one whose satisfaction or failure can be judged separately from a sibling effect.

Therefore:

    one source sentence may create several REQUIRE identities;
    one acceptance event may create several REQUIRE identities;
    one implementation artifact may realize several REQUIRE identities;
    several artifacts may contribute to one REQUIRE identity.

J1 identity is not determined by future file, module, service, branch, procedure or deployment grouping.

SEQUENCE remains separate when order itself is a governing consequence.

Evidence, qualification and activation conditions are completion conditions, not additional requirement identities merely because they are separate facts.

## 6. Thin accepted machine-consequence grammar

The seven surviving cross-domain governing forms are:

    REQUIRE
    PROHIBIT
    GATE
    AUTHORIZE
    DEFER
    LIFECYCLE
    SEQUENCE

These remain the thin operational grammar.

### REQUIRE

An accepted required effect exists.

It binds:

    accepted_effect_id
    exact subject/scope
    responsible domain/owner where applicable
    effective boundary
    realization-tracking disposition

If consequential realization completion is claimed:

    completion_contract_ref

is required.

### PROHIBIT

A typed action or consequence is inadmissible within exact scope and effective boundary.

It may reference:

    until predicate
    exception authority

but free text does not drive the prohibition.

### GATE

A named transition or consequence is admissible only when referenced bounded predicates or decisions succeed.

Failure behavior must be bounded where consequential.

### AUTHORIZE

Explicit bounded permission exists for an action or phase under exact scope, authority and effective boundary.

### DEFER

An existing requirement/consequence is validly deferred under accepted authority and a bounded reactivation/expiry condition.

A generic project hold is not automatically a DEFER.

### LIFECYCLE

A prospective accepted semantic lifecycle operation exists.

General lifecycle operations may include:

    AMEND
    SUPERSEDE
    RETIRE
    REOPEN

Requirement-level semantic succession additionally uses the C7 lineage contract in Section 11.

### SEQUENCE

Order itself is accepted consequential meaning.

It binds ordered identities rather than relying on narrative ordering.

## 7. Clause admission and domain ownership

A generic operative consequence is admitted only when:

    a governing act creates or changes a machine-trackable cross-domain consequence;
    at least one admitted competency question needs it;
    a more specific accepted domain contract does not already fully own the detail;
    machine-critical semantics can be bounded by typed references/predicates/decisions;
    the structured consequence adds operational value beyond copying prose.

When one domain clearly owns the semantics:

    keep the detailed contract in that domain;
    reference its exact accepted identity/revision;
    do not duplicate its policy in generic governing semantics.

Examples include:

    WARRANT-F policy / claim / evidence / decision contracts
    research / qualification protocols
    migration / cutover contracts
    recovery procedures
    Git / integration contracts
    continuity / reconstruction contracts.

This rule is what prevents THIN_CENTRED_HYBRID_V01 from becoming a universal Project ontology.

## 8. Closed acceptance-time realization accounting

The rich-origin completeness concept survives in bounded form.

At governing acceptance, every accepted governing effect for which realization tracking may matter must receive an explicit realization disposition.

Conceptually:

    REALIZATION_TRACKED
        a persistent realization path is required

    NO_REALIZATION_REQUIRED
        the accepted effect does not itself require a realization path

There is no silent third state.

For REALIZATION_TRACKED effects:

    the stable requirement identity enters the realization-accounting set;
    it must remain visibly open, become satisfied, be validly deferred, or later be changed/retired through governed lifecycle.

NO_REALIZATION_REQUIRED is an accepted accounting decision.

It is not inferred merely because no realizer exists yet.

This is completeness accounting, not birth-time implementation grouping.

## 9. Governing completion contract

When a realization-tracked requirement can be consequentially treated as complete, J1 binds exactly one effective:

    completion_contract_ref

A completion contract is authoritative only when owned by:

    GOVERNING_ACCEPTANCE
        OR
    ACCEPTED_DOMAIN_CONTRACT.

A realizer may not create or revise the governing completion rule merely by claiming implementation coverage.

The candidate completion contract conceptually contains:

    criterion identity
    requirement identity
    completion authority / exact accepted reference
    exact revision
    required completion components
    composition rule
    evidence requirement
    qualification requirement
    activation requirement

For the currently qualified mechanism:

    composition_rule = ALL_REQUIRED

only.

Other composition semantics are not silently generalized from C6.

They require a future governed extension and qualification.

## 10. J2 natural-owner realization

Realization facts are supplied after acceptance by the natural realizing owner.

A realizer declaration binds at least:

    realizer identity
    exact artifact / implementation / procedure reference
    realizer owner
    requirement identity
    covered completion components
    exact relevant revision / digest where needed.

The model is many-to-many:

    one realizer may cover several requirements;
    several realizers may cover one requirement.

A realizer may report a self-assessment such as FULL for observability.

That label has no governing effect.

Realizers declare bounded source facts.

They do not certify the governing requirement complete.

## 11. Governed requirement lineage

Accepted historical requirement meaning is immutable.

Meaning change does not rewrite the old requirement.

Requirement lineage uses these governed relation classes:

    CARRY_FORWARD
    REPLACE
    SPLIT
    MERGE
    RETIRE

### CARRY_FORWARD

Same accepted meaning remains the same semantic identity across a new carrier/revision.

It is valid only when semantic continuity is exact.

Changed semantic digest cannot be carried forward as unchanged meaning.

CARRY_FORWARD is continuity, not a semantic-succession graph edge.

### REPLACE

One predecessor maps to one distinct successor.

### SPLIT

One predecessor maps to two or more distinct successors in one governed relation.

### MERGE

Two or more predecessors map to one successor in one governed relation.

### RETIRE

One or more predecessors close with no successor under explicit accepted retirement authority/evidence.

A partial mapping may combine successor mapping with explicit retirement of an unmapped semantic remainder.

For each accepted transition scope:

    every live predecessor must be mapped,
    explicitly retired,
    or explicitly declared unaffected.

No live predecessor silently disappears.

At one effective boundary, a predecessor may not have competing outgoing semantic-succession relations.

Intentional 1:N uses one SPLIT.

Intentional N:1 uses one MERGE.

The semantic-succession graph is acyclic.

Every transition is bound to exact authority, source/target revisions, effective boundary and provenance.

These domain-specific lineage semantics fit through the DRP-01 shared primitives:

    semantic_identity
    exact_subject_revision_binding
    provenance_descriptor
    governing_lifecycle_reference
    obligation_reference
    governed_relation_reference

without expanding the shared substrate into a universal type system.

## 12. J3 deterministic coverage and satisfaction

For the currently qualified ALL_REQUIRED mechanism:

    valid_component_coverage
        = union(valid realizer component claims)
          intersected with accepted required components.

Duplicate claims add no coverage.

Unknown component claims cause review.

A stale or unauthorized completion contract causes review.

Then:

    coverage_complete
        = every required component is covered
          under the valid accepted completion contract.

Coverage is not satisfaction.

For a current active requirement:

    requirement_satisfied
        = completion_contract_valid
          AND coverage_complete
          AND evidence_valid where required
          AND qualification_complete where required
          AND activation_effective where required
          AND NOT conflict_present.

The exact evidence, qualification and activation facts remain owned by their natural domains.

J3 derives consequence-relevant truth from those facts.

## 13. Generated realization orientation

THIN_CENTRED_HYBRID_V01 has one versioned generated orientation projection for current active requirements.

Top-level realization orientation:

    REVIEW_REQUIRED
    DEFERRED
    OPEN
    SATISFIED

Orthogonal next gap:

    REVIEW
    COVERAGE
    EVIDENCE
    QUALIFICATION
    ACTIVATION
    NONE

Derivation precedence:

    invalid source facts
    OR conflict
    OR invalid deferral
        -> REVIEW_REQUIRED / REVIEW

    else valid governed deferral
        -> DEFERRED / NONE

    else requirement_satisfied
        -> SATISFIED / NONE

    else
        -> OPEN
        + first unresolved required gap:
           COVERAGE
           EVIDENCE
           QUALIFICATION
           ACTIVATION
           otherwise REVIEW.

An authored or reported state has no authority over this projection.

Governing lifecycle stays separate.

Therefore terms such as:

    superseded
    retired
    historical
    candidate
    paused
    blocked
    migration phase

are not realization states.

C7 first resolves which requirement identities are current.

C8 then projects realization orientation over the active set.

## 14. Non-authoritative control compilation

Accepted governing semantics and current authoritative facts may be deterministically compiled into disposable control artifacts such as:

    ActionContract
    ControlObligationSet
    gate inputs
    transition requirements
    reconstruction requirements
    coverage expectations.

Rules:

    generated control has no independent semantic authority;
    compiler/version is explicit and tested;
    exact semantic and source-fact inputs are inspectable;
    stale input invalidates consequential output;
    conflict with current accepted authority fails visibly;
    the generated projection may be destroyed and rebuilt.

This gives machine execution a compact operational representation without creating a second authority source.

    source human meaning
    proposed machine consequences
    tracked/non-tracked realization disposition where relevant

    thin semantic-faithfulness review      LOW

### Models / classifiers

Models may:

    interpret incoming events into non-authoritative hypotheses
    draft J1 clauses/metadata before owner acceptance
    nominate omissions or recital leaks
    suggest realizer coverage
    explain derived decisions
    classify bounded advisory risks.

Models may not:

    create governing authority from unaccepted inference
    silently choose normative owner decisions
    override deterministic policy
    settle authority conflict by confidence
    convert advisory detector output directly into accepted semantics.

No model/provider family is selected by this architecture.

## 17. Independent detective safety net

Structured governing semantics do not eliminate detective controls.

Research 451 found the bounded semantic recital-leak detector plausible on its frozen 24-item set.

Research 458 found the detective-only baseline credible on known historical misses, while also identifying incremental control value from persistent structured obligation tracking.

Therefore the mature candidate keeps both:

    proactive accepted structured control
        for persistent visibility, lineage and admission consequences

    independent detective mechanisms
        for authoring mistakes, omissions, realization gaps and integrity regression.

Candidate detective functions include:

    semantic recital-leak detection
    realization-gap audit
    policy / claim checks
    repository integrity review.

Detector success does not promote detector inference to authority.
