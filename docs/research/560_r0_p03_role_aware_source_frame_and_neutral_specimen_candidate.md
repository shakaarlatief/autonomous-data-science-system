## 3. Contamination and source modality

The classifier marks or excludes recognizable *evaluation-design leakage* independently of source format:

- R0-P03-specific research and contemporary experiment, source-frame/harness code, checkpoints 892+, validations 244+, and MC-0030 Messages 061+.
- Current-state, Knowledge Map, Architecture Program Overview and Continuity orientation as `REVIEW_WITH_CONTAMINATION_CONTROL`. These may contain partial answers, topical organizations or R0-P03 descriptions; their treatment must be neutral and equal across arms.
- Historical W5 experiment assets as **separately handled auxiliary source**, because owner-approved Option A requires the historical **65-carrier** source world, not an implicit merge with the new primary 60+20 population.

Only explicitly reviewable text enters this *candidate* survey; other modalities may be citable context or need qualified extractors. A closed scoring snapshot must contain exactly frozen task-eligible source blobs, the same source-accurate V03 substrate, the same bounded non-topical derived orientation, and only the selected arm's authored metadata. No evaluator gets unrestricted current-repository search, comparison design papers, hidden answers or other-arm outputs.

The present exclude rules are **not the final accepted snapshot manifest**. In particular, model collaboration records, research records and orientation may contain live obligations; suppressing leakage must not silently erase task-critical evidence. Closure and source review must establish that before freeze.

## 5. Source-frame design direction without fixture freeze

The prospective next source/eligibility procedure is **role × lifecycle × authority/privacy scope**, not merely file extension or numbered path ranges. Eligible categories should distinguish:

- current governing scope and separately historical accepted/displaced decisions;
- proposals, hypotheses and unaccepted challenges;
- J2 naturally owned realizer/schema/verification/receipts, with actual ownership and observable status;
- J3 derived projections, including intentionally stale or regenerable cases;
- AO and WARRANT-F activation, admission, evidence and engineering;
- Product/Source Universe context and Project/engineering boundaries;
- architecture succession/migration/recovery/observability and historical source conditions;
- current/recent orientation only where equalized and free of task/arm leakage.

An eligibility contract must decide which of these are *task-answer sources*, which are *auxiliary evidence*, which belong only to the separate **legacy 65-carrier world**, and which are prohibited from closed-world scoring. Samples need exact source revision and a negative/ambiguity classification where authority cannot be established. No quota/label is fixed by this text.

**Future build/heldout rules:** use independent task source closure first, then deterministic role-cell coverage toward the Research 513 60-build target; freeze all arm conditions before an unobserved 20-carrier public commit-derived maintenance draw. No sealed seed/custodian is required merely to generate unpredictability. Any unavoidable deficiency in the 80-carrier budget or truly independent author/evaluator capacity must be resolved prospectively rather than silently lowering Research 513 thresholds.

The inherited B0 shared relations are not optional even if they make B2 weaker. A prospective arm-distinctness mechanism must reject B2 duplicates of accepted lineage/J2/evidence and ensure B3 does not simply copy B1's topical vocabulary; B1 receives fair vocabulary evolution and historical authoring cost is counted. Conditional B4 selection/model parity and repeated fresh-agent/evaluator decision rules remain *unfrozen* detailed fixture choices, not decisions smuggled in through this census.
