## Candidate prospective admission criteria

These criteria are a reviewable candidate, not a task-selection freeze or new scientific thresholds.

**Origin and meaning:** require a source-bound owner query **or historical owner event** with an identifiable requested action, decision or reconstruction problem and bounded information requirement. A verbatim question is not mandatory. Record the original anchor, requested scope/time and any neutral wording transformation. An accepted heading, designer-inferred topic, module docstring or retrospective predicate membership alone is insufficient. **Category evidence:** current orientation must concern an evidenced restart/current-state/authority need at the stated time. Cross-plane discovery must need a source-supported traversal between ADS Project-development knowledge/control and Product runtime/domain, with identified endpoint roles; within-Project J1/J2/J3 stratification, within-Product boundaries, build-toolchain mentions or the word Product do not suffice. Impact/evolution must need a scoped change/dependency/predecessor analysis. Exact-source/authority/recovery must need exact identity, authority or a defined recovery operation, with its required evidence available. These are category conditions, not drafted questions or answers. No rule requires every useful source association to be accepted J2 coverage; declared intent must simply remain declared intent.

**Answerability and closure:** independently establish that the required response can be grounded in admitted exact sources, including applicable authority/currentness, normative dependencies and relevant conflicts. Determine necessary closure from the information need, not all graph neighbors. Unknown closure stays on hold. Historical, superseded, proposed, implementation and derived evidence may be relevant within its actual scope; none is automatically current governing authority. Missing evidence must not be repaired by changing the requested output to an easier one.

**Distinctness:** repeated quotations, aliases, range expansions and multiple edges from one statement do not create new needs. Distinct needs may share sources or originate in one episode if their separately attested requirements are independently answerable; twelve disjoint files or twelve different owner events are not new requirements. Do not automatically collapse genuinely different dated requests solely because they concern the same subject. Ambiguous duplicate/category judgments must be resolved by frozen source-grounded criteria before ordering, not discretionary post-draw repair.
