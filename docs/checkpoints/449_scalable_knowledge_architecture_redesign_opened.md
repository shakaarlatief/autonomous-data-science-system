The opening discussion identifies four capability layers:

```text
durability
    can the knowledge survive?

    does the right knowledge enter reasoning when it matters?

```

The central scaling requirement is that the cost of acquiring sufficient project understanding should not grow proportionally with raw information volume.

The owner rejects both extremes:

```text

```
