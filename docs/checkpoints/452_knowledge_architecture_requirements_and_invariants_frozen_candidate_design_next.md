Key requirements include one repository-native bootstrap entry, broad-to-deep reconstruction, an observable reconstruction receipt, deterministic active-route reconstruction, task-relevant governing-authority preflight before consequential guidance/action, known-risk activation, explicit epistemic roles and supersession/conflict semantics, traceable synthesis, one explicit development authority, rebuildable derived indexes, deterministic pause/return semantics, fixed-budget reconstruction discipline, bounded marginal maintenance, public/private isolation, cognitive as well as structural qualification, fail-visible degraded mode and safe self-migration.

The requirements intentionally separate ordinary relevance retrieval from required-authority resolution. Semantic or vector retrieval may later improve recall, but similarity ranking alone cannot satisfy the high-consequence governing-authority safety path. Required authority must be resolved through a deterministic or otherwise verifiable route, or the system must fail visibly.

The requirements also make source-of-truth semantics explicit. Graphs, databases, embeddings, generated indexes, caches and reconstruction plans remain fully open candidate mechanisms, but a derived representation cannot become an accidental second truth. Rebuildable state may accelerate reconstruction; unique accepted synthesis must be promoted durably if the project intends to preserve it.

Scale is now testable rather than rhetorical. Candidate qualification must use fixed reconstruction budgets and include 5x and 10x corpus-growth scenarios. Required cold-start cost may not be structurally proportional to accumulated history, and ordinary artifact creation may not require global manual maintenance proportional to corpus size.
