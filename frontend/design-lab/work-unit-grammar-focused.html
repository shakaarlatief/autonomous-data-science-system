Shape design rule under test

Different does not mean arbitrary

The true-shape family deliberately keeps the full left edge stable for reading and scanning. Category differentiation is pushed into the top, right and bottom structure. This directly incorporates the human reaction that an upper-right cut feels promising while an upper-left cut feels visually awkward.
