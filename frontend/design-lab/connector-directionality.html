ADS Cockpit Design Lab · Slice 02D · Connector Directionality

How should a relationship communicate no direction, one-way direction, or two-way direction?

Connector appearance is no longer a winner-take-all question: approved attachment treatments remain configurable. This slice isolates semantic directionality. A user's visual preferences may change how a relation is presented, but they must never change whether the underlying relation is directed.

Semantic invariant Direction belongs to the relation, not the theme
Design question

Direction should remain obvious without turning every connector into a large arrow

The four lanes use the same nodes, line geometry and interaction behavior. Only the relation's direction state changes. Chronological, causal, dependency, evidence and other relation meanings remain a later semantic-vocabulary question.

Undirected remains valid A → B supported A ← B supported A ↔ B supported User styling ≠ semantic direction
Carried-forward product decision

Connector presentation is compositional and user-configurable

Clean attachment, micro dots, frame sockets and hover attachment emphasis are retained as approved presentation mechanisms rather than forced into one universal winner. The controls below are included so directionality can be checked against those presentations without reopening the connector-style decision.

Semantic safety A directed relation must still communicate its direction. Appearance controls may not silently convert a directed relation into an undirected one.

Human review

Judge the direction grammar, not the attachment-style winner

Is the forward cue immediately readable as A → B without needing a large arrowhead?

Does reverse direction remain equally obvious when the cue moves to the opposite endpoint?

Does the bidirectional treatment read as A ↔ B rather than visual duplication or noise?

Do Micro dots and Frame sockets coexist cleanly with persistent direction cues when selected?

Should direction remain visible at rest while hover is reserved for attachment emphasis and relation highlighting?

If this grammar works, the next slice can test what relation classes such as chronology, causality, dependency, evidence or lineage should mean and how they should differ.
